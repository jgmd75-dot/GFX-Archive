Dynamics

CH57

Series 57's channel strip: a valve pre-amp with a high-pass, a passive programme EQ, a variable-mu compressor and the output iron, at 2x.

Overview

CH57 is the Series 57 counterpart of CH81: one channel of a late-50s valve desk.

PRE is a valve pre-amp: GAIN drives it (level matched, so more GAIN means more colour at about the same level), with a high-pass. PROGRAM EQ is a passive programme equaliser with its make-up valve: a low shelf with separate BOOST and ATTEN (both at once give the famous shelf with a dip above it), a broad high boost with its BANDWIDTH, and a HI-CUT. The COMPRESSOR is a variable-mu valve stage whose ratio rises with the reduction. The output transformer (IRON) and the FADER finish the channel. ORDER swaps the EQ and the compressor. The whole channel runs at 2x oversampling.

Reach for it for

  • Valve colour and control on vocals
  • Warm bass and drums
  • A complete period channel in one plug-in
  • The low boost-and-cut trick on kick and bass

Getting started

  1. Insert CH57 on a track. It opens on Init: a little pre-amp colour and gentle compression.
  2. Set GAIN for the pre-amp's colour and the HIGH-PASS to clear the lows.
  3. Shape the tone with the PROGRAM EQ: try LOW BOOST and ATTEN together.
  4. Lower THRESHOLD until the GR needle moves 3 - 6 dB; pick a RECOVERY.
  5. Set IRON for the transformer's weight and the FADER for the level.

PRE

The IN button next to the title switches the pre-amp in or out (each section has one; changes fade out and back in).

IN
An edgewise meter on the input, -20 to +3 VU.
GAIN
The valve pre-amp's drive, 0 - 30 dB (10 dB). Level matched.
HIGH-PASS
OFF, 40, 80 or 160 Hz, 12 dB per octave.
TRIM
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.

PROGRAM EQ

LOW FREQ
30, 60 or 100 Hz (60 Hz).
LOW BOOST
A low shelf, 0 - 10 (0).
LOW ATTEN
A cut, set a little higher than the boost, 0 - 10 (0): with both up, a deep shelf with a dip above it.
HI-CUT
The top shelf's frequency: 5, 10 or 20 kHz (10 kHz).
HIGH FREQ
3, 5, 8, 10, 12 or 16 kHz (10 kHz).
HIGH BOOST
A peak at HIGH FREQ, 0 - 10 (0).
BANDWIDTH
The peak's width, sharp (0) to broad (10) (5).
ATTEN
The cut above HI-CUT, 0 - 10 (0).

COMPRESSOR

GR
An edgewise gain-reduction meter.
THRESHOLD
-30 to 0 dB (-12 dB). The ratio rises with the reduction.
RECOVERY
FAST (0.3 s), MED (1 s) or SLOW (3 s).
EQ > COMP / COMP > EQ
The order of the EQ and the compressor. Its LED flashes when clicked.

OUTPUT

OUT
An edgewise meter on the output.
IRON
The output transformer, 0 - 100% (30%): the lows saturate first and the top softens.
FADER
The channel's level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
DRIFT
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): the valves' bias wanders (the pre-amp's colour, the compressor's threshold) and the two channels never quite match.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.

The signal path

The line along the foot: IN, PRE, EQ, COMP (or COMP, EQ), OUT.

Two faces

Every Series 57 unit has two faces, chosen with the ULM / COPENHAGEN keys in the header. Each changes the look and the sound, and the two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. Presets leave the face as it is.

ULM
A tighter pre-amp, narrower and more precise EQ curves, a quicker, firmer compressor.
COPENHAGEN
A warmer pre-amp (more even harmonics), broader EQ curves, a slower, softer compressor and a richer iron.

Presets

A preset sets the PRE (not TRIM or GUARD), the PROGRAM EQ, the COMPRESSOR, the order, IRON and FADER; the face, DRIFT and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Tips

  • The pre-amp is level matched: turn GAIN for colour, then set the FADER.
  • LOW BOOST and ATTEN together at 30 or 60 Hz give weight without mud.
  • COMP > EQ lets the EQ shape the compressed sound; EQ > COMP makes the compressor react to your EQ.
  • COPENHAGEN suits vocals and acoustic instruments; ULM keeps drums and guitars tight.
