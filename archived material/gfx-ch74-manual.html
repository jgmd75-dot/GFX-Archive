Dynamics

CH74

Series 74's desk channel: preamp, four-band EQ, gate and compressor, with one switch for the character of all three.

Overview

CH74 is one channel of the mixing desk in an outside-broadcast truck, and the Series 74 counterpart of CH57. It has a preamp with a high-pass, a four-band EQ, a gate and a compressor, in either order, with a fader and a MIX at the end.

The model switch sets the character of the whole channel. OB is Drive74's preamp (transformers and a class-A stage), an EQ coupled by transformers with an inductor low shelf and broad, slightly interactive curves, and a punchy VCA compressor with a diode-bridge colour. CLEAN is Drive74's balanced line driver, an accurate parametric EQ and a transparent RMS compressor with a clean gate.

The channel runs at 2x oversampling. Every GAIN setting is level matched, and Init plays at the same level in both models, so the switch changes the character, not the loudness.

Reach for it for

  • A complete channel on vocals, speech and presenters
  • Drums and bass that want transformer colour and a punchy compressor
  • A transparent channel for clean sources and music buses
  • Parallel compression with MIX

Getting started

  1. Insert CH74 on a track. It opens on Init: OB, 12 dB of GAIN, the EQ flat and the compressor not yet working.
  2. Set GAIN for colour and use the yellow HIGH-PASS keys to clear the lows.
  3. Shape the sound with the four EQ bands; the window shows the curve.
  4. Bring THRESHOLD down until the GR meter moves, pick a RATIO, and leave RELEASE on AUTO to start with.
  5. Switch to CLEAN for a transparent channel, and set the level with FADER.

Controls

The header

◄ / ►
Step to the previous or next preset.
Preset display
Shows the name of the preset in use. New instances open on Init.
OB / CLEAN
The model: the character of the unit (see Two models). Presets may set it.
S / M / L
Panel size: small, medium or large. The panel also scales to fit the plugin window.
?
Opens a pop-up with the design credit, the GFX website address and the address of this manual.

PRE

The ON / OFF keys on the header strip switch the section in or out; the change fades out and back in.

IN meter
An edgewise meter, lit amber on OB and orange-red on CLEAN: the level coming into the channel.
GAIN
The preamp gain, 0 - 36 dB (12 dB). Level matched: on OB it adds colour, on CLEAN it stays clean until the very top.
HIGH-PASS
Yellow keys (the colour code for a high-pass): OFF, 40, 80 or 160 Hz, 12 dB per octave, before the preamp. On OB with a slight lift at the corner; on CLEAN a flat Butterworth.
TRIM
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.
POL
Inverts the polarity. The change fades out and back in.

EQ

The ON / OFF keys on the header strip switch the EQ in or out. The knobs follow the house colour code: LF brown, LMF blue, HMF green, HF red; each band's GAIN and FREQ share its colour.

LF
A low shelf: GAIN -15 to +15 dB (0 dB), FREQ 30 - 450 Hz (100 Hz). On OB an inductor shelf with a small bump above the corner that thickens and rounds as it is pushed.
LMF
A peak: GAIN -15 to +15 dB (0 dB), FREQ 80 Hz - 2 kHz (400 Hz).
HMF
A peak: GAIN -15 to +15 dB (0 dB), FREQ 600 Hz - 10 kHz (3 kHz).
HF
A high shelf: GAIN -15 to +15 dB (0 dB), FREQ 1.5 - 16 kHz (8 kHz). Broad and gentle on OB.
MID Q
BROAD or NARROW for the two peaks (BROAD). On OB the Q is proportional: the more a peak boosts or cuts, the narrower it gets; on CLEAN it stays where it is set.
The curve
The EQ's response, 20 Hz to 20 kHz. On OB the bands interact slightly, as on a transformer-coupled desk: a boosted mid pulls its shelf's corner and takes a little of its gain.

DYNAMICS

The ON / OFF keys on the header strip switch the gate and the compressor in or out.

GR meter
The compressor's gain reduction.
THRESHOLD
The compressor's threshold, -40 to 0 dB (0 dB, where it does almost nothing). There is no automatic make-up: set the level with FADER.
RELEASE
The release, 30 ms - 2 s (250 ms). Its label greys while AUTO is on.
AUTO
Programme-dependent release (on by default): fast after short peaks, slow under sustained compression.
RATIO
1.5, 2, 4, 8 or 20 : 1 (4 : 1).
GATE
The gate's threshold, -80 to 0 dB (fully down is OFF). On OB a soft 1:2 expander with up to 20 dB of range; on CLEAN a clean gate with hysteresis and a short hold. Its LED lights while it closes.
ORDER
EQ FIRST (the default) or COMP FIRST. The change fades out and back in.

OUTPUT

MIX and FADER are faders: drag the cap, or click the slot to jump it there; SHIFT for fine moves, double-click for the default. The latency is 31 samples, reported to REAPER.

OUT meter
The level leaving the channel, before the fader.
FADER
The output level on a fader, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
MIX
A fader with a blue cap, dry to processed, 0 - 100% (100%): the whole channel against the dry signal, delayed to match. Below 100% it is parallel compression.
WANDER
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on OB the preamp's bias and gain and the VCA's threshold wander slowly and the channels never quite match; on CLEAN only a trace of gain wander remains.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.

Two models

Every Series 74 unit has two models, chosen with the OB / CLEAN lenses in the header. OB is the heavy mobile field sound: transformers, saturation and colour. CLEAN is the high-headroom, transparent broadcast path. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel changes with the model: OB is an olive-drab flight case with gunmetal knobs and amber lenses; CLEAN is a broadcast desk, with light-grey modules, red-capped aluminium knobs, cream keys that light red and cream VU windows.

OB
The mobile desk: Drive74's transformers and class-A stage, a transformer-coupled EQ with an inductor low shelf and proportional-Q mids, a punchy peak-sensing VCA compressor with a diode-bridge colour that grows as it works, and a soft expander.
CLEAN
Telemetry-clean: Drive74's balanced line driver, an accurate constant-Q parametric EQ, a transparent RMS compressor with a soft knee and a slower attack, and a clean gate with hysteresis and hold.

Presets

A preset sets the PRE, EQ and DYNAMICS sections (their ON / OFF keys too), ORDER, MIX, FADER and the model; TRIM, GUARD, POLARITY, WANDER and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Tips

  • Switch between OB and CLEAN on Init to hear the character change at the same level; with the compressor working, the two respond differently, just as two desks would.
  • There is no automatic make-up gain: as THRESHOLD comes down, bring FADER up to match.
  • COMP FIRST lets the EQ shape the sound after the compressor has levelled it, so boosts stay where you put them; EQ FIRST makes the compressor react to the EQ.
  • Pull MIX down for parallel compression: Parallel Punch shows how.
