Modulation & space

DLY57

Series 57's delay: a multi-head echo - a record head and four replay heads round a magnetic drum, any of them in, with swell, wow and a valve record amp.

Overview

DLY57 is the Series 57 counterpart of DLY81: a multi-head echo of the late 50s. One recording head and four replay heads sit round a turning drum of magnetic tape. Switch any of them in: one setting gives a single slap, another a rhythm of repeats.

TIME sets the delay at head 4; heads 1, 2 and 3 sit at a quarter, a half and three quarters of it. SYNC locks head 4 to the tempo. SWELL feeds every head that is in back onto the drum, through the replay EQ (TONE). The valve record amp runs oversampled 2x.

Reach for it for

  • Rhythmic multi-tap echoes from one setting
  • Slapback and doubling
  • Swelling, self-oscillating echo textures
  • Wide ping-pong repeats with SPREAD

Getting started

  1. Insert DLY57 on a track, or on a send with MIX at 100%. It opens on Init: heads 1 and 3 at 480 ms.
  2. Switch heads in and out with the HEAD keys (or click the heads on the drum): the times are shown beside the keys.
  3. Set TIME, or press SYNC and choose a note value for head 4.
  4. Raise SWELL for more repeats; TONE makes them darker or brighter.
  5. Set SPREAD for the stereo picture and MIX for the balance.

DRUM · HEADS

The drum
The record head (R) and the four replay heads, lit when in; click a head to switch it.
HEAD 1 - 4
Switch each replay head in or out; the times in ms are shown beside them.
TIME
The delay at head 4, 80 - 1200 ms (480 ms); the others at a quarter, a half and three quarters. Changes glide, as the motor speeds up or slows down.
SYNC
Locks head 4 to the tempo; the small knob picks 1/16, 1/8, dotted 1/8, 1/4 (the default), dotted 1/4, 1/2 or one bar.

ECHO

RECORD
An edgewise meter: the level on the drum.
SWELL
The feedback, 0 - 100% (35%): every head that is in feeds back. Near the top the echoes swell and run away, as they did.
TONE
The repeats get darker (-) or brighter (+) each time round (0).
SPREAD
Heads 1 and 3 go left, 2 and 4 right, 0 - 100% (60%); 0 = all centred.
WOW
The motor's wander, 0 - 100% (15%).
SATURATION
How hard the valve record amp drives the drum, 0 - 100% (30%). Oversampled 2x.

IN · OUT

INPUT
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.
MIX
Dry to echo, 0 - 100% (30%). The dry stays at full level up to 50%.
OUTPUT
The output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
DRIFT
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): the motor's speed wanders, so the repeats breathe in time and pitch.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). As a mix tool it passes straight through otherwise.

Two faces

Every Series 57 unit has two faces, chosen with the ULM / COPENHAGEN keys in the header. Each changes the look and the sound, and the two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. Presets leave the face as it is.

ULM
The kitchen-radio face: sand with olive-green keys and accents. A crisp replay, a firm record amp, the motor steady.
COPENHAGEN
Darker, rounder repeats with more low-mid body, a softer record amp and a wider wow.

Presets

A preset sets the time, sync, heads, SWELL, TONE, WOW, SATURATION, SPREAD and MIX; the face, INPUT, GUARD, OUTPUT, DRIFT and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Tips

  • Heads 1 + 3 or 2 + 4 give even rhythms; 1 + 4 or 3 + 4 give swung ones.
  • Turn TIME while the echoes ring for pitch bends, as on the real machines.
  • With SWELL past 90%, ride it like an instrument: up for a swell, down to stop it.
  • COPENHAGEN's darker repeats sit behind a vocal; ULM's crisp ones suit guitars.
