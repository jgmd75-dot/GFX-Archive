DLY74

Series 74's delay: a companded bucket-brigade echo with clock bleed, or pristine digital lines, on one switch.

Overview

DLY74 is the stereo delay of Series 74, the OB remote and broadcast series. It has two models on one switch.

OB is a bucket-brigade echo: two 4096-stage chips per side, with a compander that squeezes the signal 2:1 on the way in and expands it 1:2 on the way out, and breathes as it does. The filters before and after the chips close as the delay gets longer and the clock slows, so long echoes turn dark and gritty; the clock bleeds faintly through, its whine following the delay time; the loop saturates gently and a slight wow is always there. Moving TIME bends the pitch, as a real bucket-brigade line does. CLEAN is a pair of pristine digital lines: full bandwidth and interpolated, with a change of TIME crossfaded to the new time, so nothing bends.

MONO, STEREO and PING-PONG set how the two sides are fed. HIGH CUT and LOW CUT sit in the feedback loop, so each repeat is filtered again. A soft limit in the loop keeps even full feedback stable. The two models are level matched.

Reach for it for

  • Slapback and echo on vocals, guitars and speech
  • Dark, gritty analogue repeats for dub throws
  • Clean, tempo-synced ping-pong on synths and keys
  • Warbling, chorused echoes with MOD

Getting started

  1. Insert DLY74 on a track or a send. It opens on Init: OB, 375 ms, stereo, 30% mix.
  2. Set the time with TIME, or press SYNC and pick a note value to lock it to the tempo.
  3. Set how many repeats with FEEDBACK, and shape them with HIGH CUT and LOW CUT.
  4. Pick MONO, STEREO or PING-PONG, and set the spread of the repeats with WIDTH.
  5. Switch to CLEAN for pristine repeats, and blend the echo with MIX.

Controls

The header

PRESETS
Opens the preset list (a click on the preset display does the same). The list ends with Reset all to defaults.
◄ / ►
Step to the previous or next preset.
Preset display
Shows the name of the preset in use. New instances open on Init.
OB / CLEAN
The model: the character of the unit (see Two models). Presets may set it.
S / M / L
Panel size: small, medium or large. The panel also scales to fit the plugin window.
?
Opens a pop-up with the design credit, the GFX website address and the address of this manual.

INPUT

IN / OUT meters
Edgewise meters, lit amber on OB and orange-red on CLEAN: the level coming in and the level going out.
TRIM
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.

TIME

TIME
The delay time, 20 - 2000 ms (375 ms). On OB a change bends the pitch while the line catches up; on CLEAN it crossfades to the new time in 50 ms. Its label greys while SYNC is on.
SYNC
Locks the time to the project tempo (off by default). The time it gives shows under the note lenses.
Note lenses
1/16, 1/8T, 1/8, 1/8D, 1/4T, 1/4, 1/4D, 1/2 and BAR (1/8D): the note value for SYNC. Clicking one also switches SYNC on. The synced time is held between 20 ms and 2 s.
MODE
MONO (the input summed, one echo in the middle), STEREO (left and right, each feeding itself) or PING-PONG (the input summed into the left, the repeats crossing from side to side). STEREO by default. The change fades the echo out and back in.

REPEATS

FEEDBACK
How much of the echo goes round again, 0 - 100% (35%). Near the top the repeats run on; a soft limit in the loop keeps it stable (OB saturates gently, CLEAN limits only near full scale).
HIGH CUT
A low-pass in the feedback loop (a black cap, the colour code for a low-pass), 1 - 20 kHz (9 kHz): each repeat is darker than the last.
LOW CUT
A high-pass in the feedback loop (a yellow cap, the colour code for a high-pass), 20 - 1000 Hz (60 Hz): each repeat is thinner than the last.

MOD · CLOCK

MOD
The delay's modulation, 0 - 100% (20%): a slow wobble in time and pitch, the two sides a quarter turn apart. Mainly OB, where it is the clock's wow on top of the slight wow that is always there; on CLEAN only a trace.
CLOCK BLEED
How much of the bucket-brigade clock leaks into the echo, 0 - 100% (30%): a faint whine that follows the delay time (it is out of hearing on short delays and drops into the treble on long ones) and breathes with the compander. OB only: on CLEAN it is out of the path and its label greys.

OUTPUT

MIX and OUTPUT are desk faders: drag the cap, or click the slot to jump it there; double-click for the default.

WIDTH
The stereo width of the repeats, 0 - 100% (100%): 0 puts them all in the middle. It has no effect on MONO.
WANDER
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on OB the clock drifts and the compander's tracking wanders, the two sides never quite matched; on CLEAN only a trace of time wander remains.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.
MIX
A fader with a blue cap: dry to echo, 0 - 100% (30%), as a send: the dry stays at full level up to 50%, and the echo is at full level from 50%.
OUTPUT
A fader: the output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).

Two models

Every Series 74 unit has two models, chosen with the OB / CLEAN lenses in the header. OB is the heavy mobile field sound: transformers, saturation and colour. CLEAN is the high-headroom, transparent broadcast path. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel changes with the model: OB is an olive-drab flight case with gunmetal knobs and amber lenses; CLEAN is a broadcast desk, with light-grey modules, red-capped aluminium knobs, cream keys that light red and cream VU windows.

OB
A bucket-brigade echo with a compander: the filters close and the grit grows as the time gets longer, the clock whine follows the time, the loop saturates gently, a slight wow, and TIME bends the pitch.
CLEAN
Pristine digital lines: full bandwidth, interpolated, a change of time crossfaded with no pitch bend, a clean loop with a soft limit near full scale. CLOCK BLEED is out of the path and MOD is reduced to a trace.

Presets

A preset sets TIME, SYNC and its note, FEEDBACK, the mode, HIGH CUT, LOW CUT, MOD, CLOCK BLEED, WIDTH, MIX, OUTPUT and the model; TRIM, GUARD, WANDER and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Tips

  • Turn TIME slowly on OB while the echo plays: the repeats bend in pitch, as a real bucket-brigade line does. On CLEAN the same move just crossfades.
  • On OB the longer the time, the darker and grittier the echo, and the lower the clock whine: for bright long echoes, use CLEAN.
  • With FEEDBACK high, pull HIGH CUT down and LOW CUT up: the repeats fade into the distance instead of building up.
  • On a send, set MIX to 100% so only the echo comes back.
