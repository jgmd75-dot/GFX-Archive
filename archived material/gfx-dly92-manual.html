Modulation & space

DLY92

Series 92's studio delay: a valve-driven tape echo, or a 16-bit digital delay with tap tempo and hold, on one switch.

Overview

DLY92 is the stereo delay of Series 92: high-end home recording from the 90s, the rack outboard and DSP boxes of a serious home studio of the decade. It has two models on one switch.

ANALOG is the boutique tape echo of the decade's revival: a valve record amp onto a tape loop. The record amp saturates softly and a little unevenly, and keeps the loop in check; the tape's bandwidth follows its speed, so the top falls away with each repeat, and more so on long times; the playback head adds its bump in the lows; wow and flutter wobble the pitch; and moving TIME bends the pitch as the varispeed motor catches up. DIGITAL is the 90s digital delay: a 16-bit sampling line with its converters' band limit on the repeats, a change of TIME crossfaded to the new time, MOD as a clean chorus on the repeats, and HOLD to freeze the line and loop what is in it.

TAP sets the time from two taps, SYNC locks it to the tempo, and the LCD shows it in ms (with the note value under SYNC). MONO, STEREO and PING-PONG set how the two sides are fed. HIGH CUT and LOW CUT sit in the feedback loop, so each repeat is filtered again, and even full feedback stays stable. The two models are level matched.

Reach for it for

  • Tape slapback and warm, darkening echoes on vocals and guitars
  • Wobbling, saturated dub throws that run on
  • Clean, tempo-synced or tapped ping-pong on synths and keys
  • Frozen loops and chorused digital repeats

Getting started

  1. Insert DLY92 on a track or a send. It opens on Init: ANALOG, 375 ms, stereo, 30% mix.
  2. Set the time with TIME, tap it in with TAP, or press SYNC and pick a note value to lock it to the tempo.
  3. Set how many repeats with FEEDBACK, and shape them with HIGH CUT and LOW CUT.
  4. Pick MONO, STEREO or PING-PONG, and set the spread of the repeats with WIDTH.
  5. Switch to DIGITAL for clean 16-bit repeats and HOLD, and blend the echo with the MIX fader.

Controls

The header

PRESETS
Opens the preset list (a click on the preset display does the same). The list ends with Reset all to defaults.
◄ / ►
Step to the previous or next preset.
Preset display
Shows the name of the preset in use. New instances open on Init.
ANALOG / DIGITAL
The model: the character of the unit (see Two models). Presets may set it.
S / M / L
Panel size: small, medium or large. The panel also scales to fit the plugin window.
?
Opens a pop-up with the design credit, the GFX website address and the address of this manual.

INPUT

IN / OUT ladders
LED ladders, -30 to +6 (0 = 0 VU): the level coming in and the level going out.
TRIM
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.

TIME

The LCD shows the delay time in ms; under SYNC it shows the note value too, and HOLD when the line is frozen.

TIME
The delay time, 20 - 2000 ms (375 ms). On ANALOG a change bends the pitch while the varispeed motor catches up; on DIGITAL it crossfades to the new time in 50 ms. Its label greys while SYNC is on.
TAP
Tap twice in time: TIME is set to the gap between the taps (up to 2 s). A tap switches SYNC off.
SYNC
Locks the time to the project tempo (off by default).
Note keys
1/16, 1/8T, 1/8, 1/8D, 1/4T, 1/4, 1/4D, 1/2 and BAR (1/8D): the note value for SYNC. Pressing one also switches SYNC on. The synced time is held between 20 ms and 2 s.
MODE
MONO (the input summed, one echo in the middle), STEREO (left and right, each feeding itself) or PING-PONG (the input summed into the left, the repeats crossing from side to side). STEREO by default. The change fades the echo out and back in.

REPEATS

FEEDBACK
How much of the echo goes round again, 0 - 100% (35%). Near the top the repeats run on; the valve record amp (ANALOG) or a soft limit near full scale (DIGITAL) keeps the loop stable.
HIGH CUT
A low-pass in the feedback loop (a black cap, the colour code for a low-pass), 1 - 20 kHz (9 kHz): each repeat is darker than the last.
LOW CUT
A high-pass in the feedback loop (a yellow cap, the colour code for a high-pass), 20 - 1000 Hz (60 Hz): each repeat is thinner than the last.

MOD · HOLD

MOD
The modulation of the repeats, 0 - 100% (20%). ANALOG: wow and flutter - a slow, uneven wow that wanders with the motor and a faster capstan flutter. DIGITAL: a clean sine chorus on the repeats, the two sides a quarter turn apart.
HOLD
DIGITAL only: freezes the line - the input stops going in and what is in the line loops on, untouched, at the time it was held (off by default). It glides in and out; TIME has no effect while it is on. Greyed on ANALOG.

OUTPUT

MIX and OUTPUT are desk faders: drag the cap, or click the slot to jump it there; double-click for the default.

WIDTH
The stereo width of the repeats, 0 - 100% (100%): 0 puts them all in the middle. It has no effect on MONO.
DRIFT
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on ANALOG the capstan motor's speed wanders, each side its own, and the record valve's bias drifts as it warms; on DIGITAL the clock is steady, and only a trace of time wander remains.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.
MIX
A fader with a blue cap: dry to echo, 0 - 100% (30%), as a send: the dry stays at full level up to 50%, and the echo is at full level from 50%.
OUTPUT
A fader: the output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).

Two models

Every Series 92 unit has two models, chosen with the ANALOG / DIGITAL keys in the header - the two halves of a serious 90s home studio. ANALOG is the boutique outboard: valve, opto and transformer colour. DIGITAL is the first affordable 16 / 20-bit converters and DSP: clean, precise and a little glassy. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel lamps glow amber on ANALOG and green on DIGITAL.

ANALOG
A valve-driven tape echo: soft, uneven saturation in the loop, the top falling away with each repeat (more on long times), a head bump in the lows, wow and flutter, and TIME bending the pitch like a varispeed motor. HOLD is out of the path.
DIGITAL
A 90s 16-bit digital delay: a dithered 16-bit line, the converters' band limit on the repeats, time changes crossfaded with no pitch bend, MOD as a clean chorus on the repeats, HOLD to freeze the line, and a clean loop with a soft limit near full scale.

Presets

A preset sets TIME, SYNC and its note, FEEDBACK, the mode, HIGH CUT, LOW CUT, MOD, WIDTH, MIX, OUTPUT and the model, and switches HOLD off; TRIM, GUARD, DRIFT and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Init
ANALOG, 375 ms, stereo, 35% feedback.
Vocal Slap
A short mono tape slapback with a single repeat.
Ballad Echo
ANALOG on the quarter note, synced, thinned and darkened for a ballad vocal.
Dub Tape Loop
Long, dark tape repeats on a dotted quarter, high feedback, more wow.
Warped Tape
ANALOG with heavy wow and flutter: a seasick, warbling echo.
Runaway Tape
Tape ping-pong at full feedback, mixed low: the repeats run on and saturate.
Indie Guitar
Tape ping-pong on a dotted eighth, synced.
Clean Quarter
DIGITAL on the quarter note, synced.
Dotted Eighths
DIGITAL ping-pong on a dotted eighth, bright and full bandwidth.
Chorus Repeats
DIGITAL at 280 ms with the chorus well up on the repeats.
Ambient Ping-Pong
DIGITAL ping-pong on the half note, long and dark: a wash for pads.
Snare Throw
DIGITAL on the quarter note with a high low cut: a thin throw for snare or vocal ends.
Reset all to defaults
(Last in the list.) Every control back to its starting value.

Tips

  • Turn TIME slowly on ANALOG while the echo plays: the repeats bend in pitch as the varispeed motor catches up. On DIGITAL the same move just crossfades.
  • On ANALOG the longer the time, the slower the tape and the darker the repeats: for bright long echoes, use DIGITAL.
  • On DIGITAL, play a phrase, then press HOLD: it loops on under whatever you play next, until you release it.
  • On a send, set MIX to 100% so only the echo comes back.
