Amps & drive

Drive57

The first of Series 57: two valve stages and an output transformer, oversampled - a triode or pentode preamp into a summing valve whose supply sags, through iron that saturates the lows first.

Overview

Drive57 is the first plug-in of Series 57, a family in the spirit of mid-century modernist hi-fi and valve studio gear: alabaster enamel, charcoal lines, cream knobs, signal-orange accents and edgewise meters in amber windows.

It is a valve drive in two stages. V1, the PREAMP, gives the gain: a TRIODE (soft, uneven clipping - even harmonics, warmth) or a PENTODE (more gain and a sharper, more even knee - odd harmonics, bite). V2, the SUMMING valve, follows through a coupling capacitor with its own DRIVE and its own triode or pentode; its supply SAGs under load, so the stage breathes and compresses as it is pushed. Then the OUTPUT IRON: a transformer whose core saturates the lows first and softens the top.

BIAS sets both valves' operating point, from COLD to HOT. Everything non-linear runs at 2x or 4x oversampling, and every stage is level matched, so turning DRIVE changes the colour far more than the loudness.

Reach for it for

  • Warmth and density on a bus or the whole mix
  • Valve colour on vocals, bass and keys
  • Crunch and grit, from a hint to full meltdown
  • Parallel drive with MIX
  • Fattening lows with transformer saturation

Getting started

  1. Insert Drive57 on a track or bus. It opens on Init: a gentle triode warmth.
  2. Play the track and watch the IN meter: aim for the needle around -10 to 0. Use INPUT to get there (GUARD eases very hot sources down).
  3. Turn up V1's DRIVE for colour; V2's DRIVE for density and glue. The valves glow brighter the harder they are driven.
  4. Flip a valve to PENTODE for more bite; set BIAS towards HOT for a warmer, earlier break-up, or COLD for clean with a sudden edge.
  5. Shape the result with IRON and TONE, and blend it with MIX.

Controls

The header

PRESETS
Opens the preset list (a click on the preset display does the same). The list ends with Reset all to defaults.
◄ / ►
Step to the previous or next preset.
Preset display
Shows the number and name of the preset in use. New instances open on Init.
ULM / COPENHAGEN
The face: the look and the voice of the unit (see Two faces). Presets leave it as it is.
S / M / L
Panel size: small, medium or large. The panel also scales to fit the plugin window.
?
Opens a pop-up with the design credit, the GFX website address and the address of this manual.

LEVEL

IN / OUT
Edgewise meters, -20 to +3 VU, in amber windows. 0 VU is about -18 dBFS RMS.
INPUT
Trim before the valves, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down before they reach the valves (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.

V1 PREAMP · V2 SUMMING

TRIODE / PENTODE
Each valve's type. The triode clips softly and unevenly - even harmonics; the pentode has more gain and a sharper, more symmetrical knee - odd harmonics. The orange dot marks the one in use; changing it fades the sound out and back in.
V1 DRIVE
The preamp's drive, 0 - 30 dB (8 dB).
V2 DRIVE
The summing valve's drive, 0 - 24 dB (4 dB). Its supply SAGs with the signal driving it.

SUPPLY · IRON · OUTPUT

BIAS
Both valves' operating point, COLD to HOT (50%). COLD: cleaner, then a sudden edge. HOT: earlier, warmer break-up with more even harmonics.
SAG
How far V2's supply droops under load, 0 - 100% (30%): its headroom and bias follow the signal, so loud passages compress and bloom.
IRON
The output transformer, 0 - 100% (40%): the core saturates the lows first and the top softens.
TONE
A tilt about 900 Hz, DARK to BRIGHT, -6 to +6 dB (0).
MIX
Dry to driven, 0 - 100% (100%). The dry signal is delayed to match, so parallel blends stay phase-aligned.
OUTPUT
The output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
OVERSAMPLING 2x / 4x
The valves and the iron run at 2x or 4x (the default) the sample rate, with linear-phase filters. Both have the same latency (37 samples, reported to REAPER), so changing it only fades the sound out and back in. 4x is cleaner on hard drive; 2x uses about half the CPU.
DRIFT
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): the heaters wander - each valve's bias and gain drift slowly, and the left and right channels never quite match. 0 is a steady, repeatable drive.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.

Two faces

Every Series 57 unit has two faces, chosen with the ULM / COPENHAGEN keys in the header. Each changes the look and the sound, and the two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in.

ULM
German studio precision, on matte alabaster enamel with signal orange. A tight, lean low end (the coupling and the iron set higher), more headroom up to a firm knee, a quick sag recovery and a slightly forward upper-mid.
COPENHAGEN
Danish home hi-fi warmth, on grey-birch enamel with sage plates, wool-lined wells and teak cheeks. A softer, earlier knee with more even harmonics (the valves biased a little hotter), rounder, blooming lows (the iron saturates lower and sooner), a soft top and a slow sag that swells rather than pumps.

The signal path

The orange line along the foot of the panel: IN, V1 PREAMP, V2 SUMMING, OUTPUT IRON, OUT.

Presets

A preset sets INPUT, the valves, the supply, the iron, TONE, MIX and OUTPUT; the face, GUARD, 2x / 4x, DRIFT and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Init
A gentle triode warmth: V1 at 8 dB, V2 at 4 dB.
Warm Glow
A little more drive, a hotter bias and more iron, tilted slightly dark.
Hi-Fi Polish
Barely driven, a cooler bias, a touch of brightness: polish for a mix bus.
Triode Bus
Both triodes, V2 pushed harder with more sag: glue and density for a bus.
Pentode Crunch
Two pentodes pushed into crunch.
Vocal Valve
A triode preamp into a pentode, a bright tilt: presence and grit for a voice.
Bass Iron
Iron near full and a dark tilt: thick, saturated lows.
Cold Bias Edge
A cold pentode preamp: clean until it isn't, then a sudden edge.
Hot Bias Bloom
A hot bias: early, warm, even-harmonic bloom.
Parallel Fire
Two pentodes pushed hard, mixed in at 35%: heat under a clean signal.
Sagging Supply
SAG near full: the supply droops and the drive breathes and pumps with the music.
Full Melt
Everything up: two pentodes driven flat out, a hot bias, heavy sag and iron.
Reset all to defaults
(Last in the list.) Every control back to its starting value.

Tips

  • Because the stages are level matched, you can judge DRIVE by its colour alone: turn it up until it sounds right, not until it sounds louder.
  • V1 sets the character, V2 the density. A lot of V1 into a little V2 is crunchy; a little V1 into a lot of V2 is thick and glued.
  • On a mix bus, keep the drive low and use SAG and IRON for movement and weight.
  • For parallel drive, push the valves hard and bring MIX down to 20 - 40%.
  • Try both faces on the same preset: ULM keeps a bass tight and forward, COPENHAGEN rounds it out and softens the top.
  • Set DRIFT to 0 for a steady, repeatable drive; leave it up for valves that are never quite the same twice.
