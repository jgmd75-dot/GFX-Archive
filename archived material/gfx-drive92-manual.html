Amps & drive

Drive92

Series 92's studio preamp: a boutique starved-plate valve stage, or a 20-bit converter front-end, on one switch.

Overview

Drive92 opens the new Series 92 units: high-end home recording from the 90s, the rack outboard and DSP boxes of a serious home studio of the decade. It is the front-end, with two models on one switch.

ANALOG is the boutique valve preamp of the time: GAIN into a valve run on a low plate voltage, so the negative half of the wave flattens early and unevenly, then an output transformer. TUBE sets the heat, from cool and polite to hot and furry; IRON sets how hard the transformer works. DIGITAL is a 20-bit converter front-end: clean right up to full scale, where it clips. SOFT LIMIT sets the knee of the converter's own soft limiter, and BITS sets its word length (16, 20 or 24 bits, dithered).

The preamp runs at 2x or 4x oversampling. Every GAIN setting is level matched in both models, and the two models are matched to each other, so GAIN and the switch change the colour, not the loudness.

Reach for it for

  • Valve warmth and thickness on vocals, bass and synths
  • The edge of 90s digital: converter overs, soft limiting and 16-bit grain
  • A clean high-pass and gain stage
  • Parallel valve drive under a clean signal

Getting started

  1. Insert Drive92 on a track. It opens on Init: ANALOG, 12 dB of GAIN.
  2. Turn GAIN for more colour; the level stays put.
  3. Set TUBE for the valve's heat and IRON for the transformer.
  4. Switch to DIGITAL for the converter: push GAIN into its ceiling and shape the knee with SOFT LIMIT; try 16 BITS for grain.
  5. Blend with the MIX fader and set the level with the OUTPUT fader.

Controls

The header

PRESETS
Opens the preset list (a click on the preset display does the same). The list ends with Reset all to defaults.
◄ / ►
Step to the previous or next preset.
Preset display
Shows the name of the preset in use. New instances open on Init.
ANALOG / DIGITAL
The model: the character of the unit (see Two models). Presets may set it.
S / M / L
Panel size: small, medium or large. The panel also scales to fit the plugin window.
?
Opens a pop-up with the design credit, the GFX website address and the address of this manual.

INPUT

IN / OUT ladders
LED ladders, -30 to +6 (0 = 0 VU): the level going into the preamp and coming out.
TRIM
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.
POLARITY
Inverts the polarity. The change fades out and back in.

PREAMP

GAIN
The preamp gain, 0 - 36 dB (12 dB). Level matched: on ANALOG it heats the valve, on DIGITAL it drives the converter towards its ceiling.
HIGH-PASS
Yellow keys (the house high-pass colour): OFF, 40, 80 or 160 Hz, 12 dB per octave, before the preamp. On ANALOG with a slight lift at the corner; on DIGITAL a flat Butterworth.

VALVE · IRON / CONVERTER

The section follows the model; its LCD shows the valve's heat (ANALOG) or the converter's word length (DIGITAL).

TUBE / SOFT LIMIT
ANALOG: the valve's heat, 0 - 100% (45%) - hotter lowers and softens the knee and brings more even harmonics. DIGITAL: the converter's soft limiter - 0 is hard digital overs, 100 a gentle knee from -6 dBFS.
IRON
ANALOG only: how hard the output transformer works, 0 - 100% (40%): the lows thicken, the top softens. Greyed on DIGITAL.
BITS
DIGITAL only: the converter's word length, 16, 20 or 24 bits (20), with TPDF dither. The change fades out and back in.

OUTPUT

TONE
A tilt about 900 Hz, -6 to +6 dB (0 dB): dark to bright.
DRIFT
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on ANALOG the valve warms up and the mains wander, so its bias and gain drift slowly and the channels never quite match; on DIGITAL only a trace of gain drift remains.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.
MIX
A fader with a blue cap: dry to processed, 0 - 100% (100%). The dry signal is delayed to match.
OUTPUT
A fader: the output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
OVERSAMPLING
2x or 4x (4x). The latency is the same in both (37 samples, reported to REAPER), so changing it only fades the sound out and back in.

Two models

Every Series 92 unit has two models, chosen with the ANALOG / DIGITAL keys in the header - the two halves of a serious 90s home studio. ANALOG is the boutique outboard: valve, opto and transformer colour. DIGITAL is the first affordable 16 / 20-bit converters and DSP: clean, precise and a little glassy. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel lamps glow amber on ANALOG and green on DIGITAL.

ANALOG
A boutique starved-plate valve preamp into an output transformer: warm, thick and furry, more so with GAIN, TUBE and IRON.
DIGITAL
A 20-bit converter front-end: clean to full scale, then the converter's ceiling, with an adjustable soft limiter before it and a choice of 16, 20 or 24 bits. IRON is out of the path.

Tips

  • Switch between ANALOG and DIGITAL at the same GAIN to hear valve against converter: the level stays the same.
  • On DIGITAL, SOFT LIMIT at 0 gives the hard edge of an over; raise it for the rounded limiting of later converters.
  • 16 BITS adds a fine grain on quiet tails; it is most audible on reverbs and fades.
  • Parallel Tube shows MIX at work: keep the clean signal and add a hot valve under it.
