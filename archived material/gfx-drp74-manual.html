Modulation & space

DRP74

Series 74's remote digital reverb: metallic, non-linear mobile rooms, or a pristine algorithmic plate, on one switch.

Overview

DRP74 is the Series 74 counterpart of DRP57: the early-80s remote digital reverb from an outside-broadcast truck, with two models on one switch.

OB is the 12-bit truck processor: an input transformer, a 13 kHz converter path with a little quantisation grit, and four algorithms. ROOM is a dense, short mobile room built from coloured delay lines with a metallic ring; NONLIN, GATE and REVERSE are the classic shaped bursts: a non-linear envelope, a flat burst with a hard close, and a rising swell. An output transformer finishes the return. CLEAN is a pristine, full-bandwidth, modulated plate with no grit; the same lenses pick a BRIGHT, MEDIUM, DARK or LONG plate.

The dry signal passes untouched, and MIX uses a send law. The return is level matched across the algorithms, the plates and DECAY, and the two models are matched to each other, so the switch changes the space, not the loudness.

Reach for it for

  • Short, metallic rooms on drums, guitars and speech
  • Non-linear and gated snare and tom sounds
  • Reverse swells into vocals and hits
  • A clean, smooth plate on vocals, or on a send at 100%

Getting started

  1. Insert DRP74 on a track, or on a send with MIX at 100%. It opens on Init: OB, ROOM, 1.6 s.
  2. Pick the algorithm with the four lenses: ROOM, NONLIN, GATE or REVERSE.
  3. Set SIZE and DECAY; on NONLIN, GATE and REVERSE, SIZE becomes LENGTH and DECAY sets the slope of the burst.
  4. Shape the return with DAMP and LOW CUT, and add PRE-DELAY to keep it off the source.
  5. Switch to CLEAN for a smooth plate, and set the level with MIX and OUTPUT.

Controls

The header

PRESETS
Opens the preset list (a click on the preset display does the same). The list ends with Reset all to defaults.
◄ / ►
Step to the previous or next preset.
Preset display
Shows the name of the preset in use. New instances open on Init.
OB / CLEAN
The model: the character of the unit (see Two models). Presets may set it.
S / M / L
Panel size: small, medium or large. The panel also scales to fit the plugin window.
?
Opens a pop-up with the design credit, the GFX website address and the address of this manual.

INPUT

IN / OUT meters
Edgewise meters, lit amber on OB and orange-red on CLEAN: the level coming in and going out.
TRIM
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.

ALGORITHM / PLATE

The module is titled ALGORITHM on OB and PLATE on CLEAN. A change of algorithm or plate fades the return out, clears the tank and fades back in.

The response window
A backlit window, amber on OB and orange-red on CLEAN, drawing the shape of the return after a click: the pre-delay, then the decay or the burst. It names the algorithm and shows the RT60 or, on NONLIN, GATE and REVERSE, the length of the window.
ROOM
OB: a dense, short mobile room. Four diffusers per side feed eight delay lines, each with a tuned all-pass that gives the metallic ring of the hardware; the room is re-quantised to 12 bits on every pass. SIZE scales the room, DECAY is its RT60.
NONLIN
OB: a densely diffused copy read by 96 taps per side, on a non-linear envelope that holds up and then falls away to nothing at the end of the window.
GATE
OB: the same taps on a flat burst with a hard close. DECAY sets how much the burst falls across the window.
REVERSE
OB: the taps on a rising ramp, then the hard stop. DECAY sets how slowly it rises.
BRIGHT / MEDIUM / DARK / LONG
CLEAN: the plate. BRIGHT, MEDIUM and DARK change the input bandwidth and the damping; LONG is a bigger plate (1.25 x SIZE, 1.5 x DECAY) with slower, wider modulation.

SPACE

PRE-DELAY
The delay before the reverb, 0 - 250 ms (20 ms). It glides when moved.
SIZE
The size of the room or the plate, 0 - 100% (50%). On NONLIN, GATE and REVERSE it reads LENGTH: the window, 80 - 640 ms (226 ms at 50%). It glides, so it can be moved while playing.
DECAY
The RT60, 0.2 - 10 s (1.6 s). On NONLIN, GATE and REVERSE it sets the slope of the burst: short falls away sooner (or, on REVERSE, rises later).
DAMP
How much sooner the highs die than the mids, 0 - 100% (40%). A black cap: the house colour for a low-pass.
LOW CUT
A 2-pole high-pass on the return, 20 - 600 Hz (80 Hz). The dry signal is untouched. A yellow cap: the house colour for a high-pass.

OUTPUT

MIX and OUTPUT are faders.

MIX
The blue fader: dry to reverb, 0 - 100% (30%). A send law: the dry stays at full level up to 50% and the reverb is at full level from 50%; use 100% on a send.
OUTPUT
The output fader: the output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
WANDER
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on OB the converter clock wanders, so every room line drifts on its own and the decay breathes; on CLEAN (a crystal clock) only a trace of decay wander remains.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.

Two models

Every Series 74 unit has two models, chosen with the OB / CLEAN lenses in the header. OB is the heavy mobile field sound: transformers, saturation and colour. CLEAN is the high-headroom, transparent broadcast path. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel changes with the model: OB is an olive-drab flight case with gunmetal knobs and amber lenses; CLEAN is a broadcast desk, with light-grey modules, red-capped aluminium knobs, cream keys that light red and cream VU windows.

OB
The 12-bit truck processor: input and output transformers, a band-limited converter path with gentle grit, and four algorithms - a metallic mobile ROOM, NONLIN, GATE and REVERSE.
CLEAN
A pristine algorithmic plate: a smooth, dense, modulated tank at full bandwidth with no grit. The lenses pick a BRIGHT, MEDIUM, DARK or LONG plate.

Presets

A preset sets PRE-DELAY, SIZE, DECAY, DAMP, LOW CUT, the algorithm or plate, MIX, OUTPUT and the model; TRIM, GUARD, WANDER and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Init
OB ROOM, 1.6 s, 20 ms of pre-delay, mixed at 30%.
Truck Room
A small, short OB room, for drums and guitars.
Snare NonLin
OB NONLIN with no pre-delay and a 150 Hz low cut, for snare.
Gated Toms
A longer OB GATE burst, for toms and big snares.
Reverse Swell
OB REVERSE over a long window, for swells into hits and vocals.
Commentary Booth
A tiny, dark OB room with a high low cut, for speech.
Stadium Tunnel
The biggest OB room with a long decay and 45 ms of pre-delay.
Metal Box
A small OB room with the damping off: the metallic ring to the fore.
Clean Vocal Plate
A MEDIUM plate with 40 ms of pre-delay, for vocals.
Bright Snare Plate
A short BRIGHT plate, for snare.
Dark Ballad Plate
A DARK plate, 2.8 s, for ballads.
Long Telemetry Plate
The LONG plate at 5 s with 60 ms of pre-delay.
Reset all to defaults
(Last in the list.) Every control back to its starting value.

Tips

  • For the classic 80s gated snare, use GATE or NONLIN with no pre-delay and a LOW CUT around 150 Hz, and set the window with LENGTH.
  • Metal Box shows the OB room's ring: turn DAMP down and keep SIZE small to hear it, or up to tame it.
  • On a send, set MIX to 100%: the return is level matched, so the send level sets how much you hear.
  • Switch between OB and CLEAN on the same settings to hear the 12-bit room against the plate: the level stays the same.
