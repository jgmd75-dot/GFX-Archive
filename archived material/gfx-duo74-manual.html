Duo74

Series 74's OB channel: a limiter and an EQ chained in either order, a character VCA pair or a transparent master broadcast processor on one switch.

Overview

Duo74 is the Series 74 counterpart of Duo57: two remote modules in one case, chained. The LIMITER and the EQ can run in either order, each with its own ON / OFF keys, and the model switch changes what both of them are.

OB is a character pair from the truck: a fast feed-back VCA limiter, punchy and a little pumping, with an iron output that saturates the lows first, into a coloured broadcast EQ with an input transformer, shelves with a little overshoot and a presence peak that broadens as it is boosted. CLEAN is an ultra-transparent master broadcast processor: a smooth RMS / peak programme limiter with look-ahead, a soft knee and a programme-dependent release, adding no harmonics, into a clean minimum-phase EQ with the same controls.

Both modules run at 2x oversampling. THRESHOLD has an automatic make-up and every THRESHOLD and RATIO is level matched, and the two models are matched to each other, so the controls change the amount of limiting and the colour, not the loudness.

Reach for it for

  • Punchy, slightly pumping limiting with iron colour on drums, vocals and buses
  • Speech and commentary: limiting with a presence lift
  • Transparent programme limiting on a mix or a broadcast feed
  • Parallel limiting under a clean signal

Getting started

  1. Insert Duo74 on a track. It opens on Init: OB, the limiter first at -12 dB and 8:1, the EQ flat.
  2. Lower THRESHOLD and watch the GAIN REDUCTION meter; the level stays put.
  3. Pick a RATIO lens, then set ATTACK and RELEASE: a slow attack lets the punch through, a short release pumps.
  4. Shape the tone with LOW, PRESENCE (and its frequency lenses) and HIGH; use ORDER to put the EQ first.
  5. Switch to CLEAN for a transparent master limiter, and blend with the MIX fader.

LIMITER

The ON / OFF keys in the header strip switch the limiter in or out; off, its knob labels grey and the change fades out and back in. The title reads VCA on OB and PROGRAMME on CLEAN.

GAIN REDUCTION
An edgewise meter, lit amber on OB and orange-red on CLEAN: the needle rests at 0 on the right and swings left as it limits.
THRESHOLD
Where limiting starts, -30 to 0 dB (-12 dB). An automatic make-up follows it, so lower settings limit harder without getting louder.
ATTACK
How quickly it clamps, 0.1 - 20 ms (1 ms). On OB a slower attack lets the transients punch through.
RELEASE
How quickly it lets go, 20 - 2000 ms (150 ms). On OB short settings pump; on CLEAN it sets the recovery after short peaks, and sustained limiting recovers more slowly.
RATIO
4, 8, 20 or LIMIT (8). Each ratio is level matched.

EQ

The ON / OFF keys in the header strip switch the EQ in or out; off, its knob labels grey and the change fades out and back in. The title reads BROADCAST on OB and MINIMUM PHASE on CLEAN. The bands follow the house colour code: LOW brown, PRESENCE green (its knob and its frequency keys), HIGH red.

LOW
The brown knob: a shelf at 100 Hz, -12 to +12 dB (0 dB). On OB with a little overshoot above the corner.
PRESENCE
The green knob: a broad peak, -12 to +12 dB (0 dB). On OB it broadens as it is boosted or cut.
PRESENCE KHZ
The green keys: the presence frequency, 0.7, 1.4, 2.8 or 5 kHz (2.8). The change fades out and back in.
HIGH
The red knob: a shelf at 10 kHz, -12 to +12 dB (0 dB).

IN · OUT

ORDER
LIMIT FIRST or EQ FIRST. Its key flashes when clicked; the change fades out and back in.
MIX
The fader with the blue cap: dry to processed, 0 - 100% (100%), for parallel limiting. The dry signal is delayed to match.
OUTPUT
The fader with the plain cap (dark on OB, cream on CLEAN): the output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
TRIM
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
WANDER
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on OB the VCA's threshold drifts and the two channels' EQ never quite match; on CLEAN only a trace of threshold wander remains.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.

Two models

Every Series 74 unit has two models, chosen with the OB / CLEAN lenses in the header. OB is the heavy mobile field sound: transformers, saturation and colour. CLEAN is the high-headroom, transparent broadcast path. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel changes with the model: OB is an olive-drab flight case with gunmetal knobs and amber lenses; CLEAN is a broadcast desk, with light-grey modules, red-capped aluminium knobs, cream keys that light red and cream VU windows.

OB
A character pair: a fast feed-back VCA limiter (punchy, a little pumping) with a VCA line amp and an iron output, and a coloured broadcast EQ with an input transformer and a soft make-up amp.
CLEAN
A transparent master broadcast processor: an RMS / peak programme limiter with look-ahead, a 10 dB soft knee and a programme-dependent release, no added harmonics, and a clean minimum-phase EQ. Latency is the same in both models (63 samples, reported to REAPER).

Presets

A preset sets the LIMITER and EQ controls, their ON / OFF keys, the order, MIX, OUTPUT and the model; TRIM, GUARD, WANDER and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Init
OB, the limiter first at -12 dB and 8:1, the EQ flat.
Commentary Box
OB, the EQ first with a 2.8 kHz lift and less low end, for speech.
Stadium PA
OB at 20:1 with a 1.4 kHz lift and a softer top, for announcements.
Pitch-side Mic
OB with the lows cut and a 5 kHz lift, for close field mics.
Crowd Bed
OB at 4:1 with a slow attack and release, for crowd and ambience.
Truck Vocal
OB, the EQ first, a fast release and a presence lift, for vocals.
Drum Bus Pump
OB at LIMIT with a 3 ms attack and a short release: punchy and pumping.
Parallel Punch
OB limited hard and mixed in at 40%.
Brass Band Remote
OB at 4:1, a little weight and air, the 1.4 kHz region eased.
Clean Master
CLEAN at LIMIT, -8 dB, a gentle lift at both ends.
Clean Speech Leveller
CLEAN at 4:1 with a long release, levelling speech.
Transmitter Safe
CLEAN at LIMIT, -6 dB, a 0.1 ms attack: a transparent safety limiter.
Reset all to defaults
(Last in the list.) Every control back to its starting value.

Tips

  • Switch between OB and CLEAN at the same settings to hear what the VCA, the iron and the transformer add: the level stays the same.
  • On OB, RELEASE is the pump: short settings breathe with the beat, long ones hold the level steady.
  • LIMIT FIRST lets the EQ shape a controlled signal; EQ FIRST makes the limiter react to the EQ, so a presence lift is held in check.
  • For parallel limiting, set a low THRESHOLD at LIMIT and bring it in with MIX, as Parallel Punch does.
