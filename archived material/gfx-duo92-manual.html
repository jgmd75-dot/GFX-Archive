Duo92

Series 92's voice channel: a compressor and an EQ in one rack - an opto and a valve-buffered passive EQ, or an early digital desk channel, on one switch.

Overview

Duo92 is the Series 92 counterpart of Duo74: the "voice channel" of a serious 90s home studio, a COMPRESSOR and an EQ in one rack. The two sections can run in either order, each with its own IN / OUT keys, and the model switch changes what both of them are.

ANALOG is the boutique outboard of the time: an opto compressor - a lamp and a light cell, so the attack is soft and the release programme dependent - into a valve make-up stage, and a valve-buffered passive EQ with broad shelves and a broad presence peak. Boosting LOW also dips the low mids just above it, as boosting and cutting together on the old passive boxes did. DIGITAL is the channel of an early digital desk: a clean RMS compressor with exact ATTACK and RELEASE, a minimum-phase parametric EQ with the same controls plus Q, and the desk's 20-bit word length.

The compressor's make-up is automatic: PEAK REDUCTION / THRESHOLD and RATIO set the amount of compression, not the level, and the two models are level matched, so the switch changes the colour, not the loudness.

Reach for it for

  • Vocals and voice-over: smooth levelling and presence in one rack
  • Bass DI and acoustic guitar that need evening out and shaping
  • Opto colour against the precision of a 90s digital desk
  • Parallel compression with the MIX fader

Getting started

  1. Insert Duo92 on a track. It opens on Init: ANALOG, the compressor first at 40% peak reduction and 4:1, the EQ flat.
  2. Turn PEAK REDUCTION and watch the GR ladder; the level stays put.
  3. Pick a RATIO key and SLOW or FAST for the opto (on DIGITAL, set ATTACK and RELEASE).
  4. Shape the tone with LOW, PRESENCE and HIGH and their frequency keys; try EQ FIRST.
  5. Blend with the MIX fader and set the level with the OUTPUT fader.

Controls

The header

PRESETS

◄ / ►
Step to the previous or next preset.
Preset display
Shows the name of the preset in use. New instances open on Init.
ANALOG / DIGITAL
The model: the character of the unit (see Two models). Presets may set it.
S / M / L
Panel size: small, medium or large. The panel also scales to fit the plugin window.
?
Opens a pop-up with the design credit, the GFX website address and the address of this manual.

INPUT

IN / OUT ladders
LED ladders, -30 to +6 (0 = 0 VU): the level going into the channel and coming out.
TRIM
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.
ORDER
COMP FIRST (the default) or EQ FIRST. The change fades out and back in.

COMPRESSOR

The title follows the model (OPTO or RMS); the IN / OUT keys in its title strip take the section out of the path (the change fades out and back in).

GR ladder
The gain reduction, 0 to -20 dB.
PEAK REDUCTION / THRESHOLD
The amount of compression: 0 to 100% on ANALOG (the threshold from 0 to -40 dB), the threshold in dB on DIGITAL (-16 dB). Clockwise compresses more on both. The make-up is automatic, so the level stays put.
ATTACK
DIGITAL only: 0.1 - 80 ms (5 ms), exact. Greyed on ANALOG.
RELEASE
DIGITAL only: 20 - 2000 ms (200 ms), exact. Greyed on ANALOG.
GAIN / MAKE-UP
A fader with a yellow cap: -12 to +12 dB (0 dB) on top of the automatic make-up. On ANALOG it also drives the valve make-up stage a little harder.
RATIO
Keys: 2, 4, 8 or LIM (4). On ANALOG the opto's slope is soft and its LIM about 12:1; on DIGITAL LIM is a true limit.
OPTO SLOW / FAST
ANALOG only: the light cell's speed (SLOW). SLOW: about 10 ms attack, the first half of the release in about 120 ms and the rest over several seconds; FAST: about 2 ms attack and a quicker release in both parts.

EQ

The title follows the model (PASSIVE or PARAMETRIC); its IN / OUT keys take it out of the path.

LOW
A brown cap: a shelf, -12 to +12 dB (0 dB). On ANALOG a broad passive shelf, and a boost also dips the low mids about 1.8 octaves above it.
LOW frequency
Brown keys: 30, 60, 100 or 200 Hz (60 Hz). The change fades out and back in.
PRESENCE
A green cap: a peak, -12 to +12 dB (0 dB). On ANALOG a broad peak; on DIGITAL its width is set by Q.
PRESENCE frequency
Green keys: 0.8, 1.5, 3 or 5 kHz (3 kHz). The change fades out and back in.
Q
DIGITAL only: the presence peak's Q, 0.4 - 4 (1). Greyed on ANALOG.
HIGH
A red cap: a shelf at 10 kHz, -12 to +12 dB (0 dB). On ANALOG a broad passive shelf.

OUTPUT

DRIFT
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on ANALOG the lamp warms and the mains wander, so the opto's threshold drifts and the two channels' EQ never quite match; on DIGITAL only a trace of threshold drift remains.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.
MIX
A fader with a blue cap: dry to processed, 0 - 100% (100%). The dry signal is delayed to match.
OUTPUT
A fader: the output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).

Two models

Every Series 92 unit has two models, chosen with the ANALOG / DIGITAL keys in the header - the two halves of a serious 90s home studio. ANALOG is the boutique outboard: valve, opto and transformer colour. DIGITAL is the first affordable 16 / 20-bit converters and DSP: clean, precise and a little glassy. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel lamps glow amber on ANALOG and green on DIGITAL.

ANALOG
An opto compressor (soft attack, programme-dependent two-part release, a wide soft knee) into a valve make-up stage, and a valve-buffered passive EQ with broad shelves, the low boost's dip above it and a broad presence peak. ATTACK, RELEASE and Q are out of the path.
DIGITAL
An early digital desk channel: a clean RMS compressor with exact ATTACK and RELEASE, a minimum-phase parametric EQ with Q, and a 20-bit word length with TPDF dither. The opto keys are out of the path.

Presets

A preset sets both sections, their IN / OUT, the ORDER, MIX, OUTPUT and the model; TRIM, GUARD, DRIFT and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Tips

  • Switch between ANALOG and DIGITAL on the same settings to hear opto and valve against the digital desk: the level stays the same.
  • On ANALOG, long compression slows the release: let a vocal sit in the opto for a phrase and the gain recovers gently afterwards.
  • Boost LOW on ANALOG for the passive trick: weight underneath with the low mids kept clear.
  • Put the EQ first to make the compressor react to the tone you have shaped, or after it to shape the compressed sound.
