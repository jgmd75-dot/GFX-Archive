Dynamics

Fet74

Series 74's studio compressor: a grabbing, pumping FET compressor, or a transparent RMS leveller, on one switch.

Overview

Fet74 is the studio compressor of Series 74, with two models on one switch.

OB is a FET peak compressor / limiter, feedback style: INPUT drives the signal into a fixed threshold, ATTACK runs from 20 to 800 microseconds, and the RATIO lenses give 4, 8, 12 or 20 : 1, or ALL - all buttons in, aggressive, pumping and dirty. The FET adds distortion that grows with the gain it takes away, then a class-A gain stage and an output transformer add their colour. It grabs and it pumps. CLEAN is a transparent RMS leveller: an RMS detector, a soft knee and a programme-dependent release, with no added harmonics. Its threshold follows the same INPUT knob, so the controls mean the same on both.

The gain cell runs at 2x oversampling. Every INPUT and RATIO setting is level matched by the make-up, and the two models are matched to each other, so INPUT changes how much it compresses, not how loud it is.

Reach for it for

  • Grabbing vocals and speech hard, with FET bite
  • Pumping drums, rooms and crowd mics with ALL
  • Transparent levelling of a voice or a bus on CLEAN
  • Parallel compression under the dry signal

Getting started

  1. Insert Fet74 on a track. It opens on Init: OB, 12 dB of INPUT at 4:1.
  2. Turn INPUT for more compression and watch the GAIN REDUCTION meter; the level stays put.
  3. Set ATTACK and RELEASE, and pick a RATIO; try ALL for the pumping all-buttons sound.
  4. Use the SIDE-CHAIN high-pass to stop the lows pulling the gain down.
  5. Switch to CLEAN for transparent levelling, and blend with MIX.

Panel size: small, medium or large. The panel also scales to fit the plugin window.

INPUT

IN / OUT meters
Edgewise meters, lit amber on OB and orange-red on CLEAN: the level coming in and the level going out.
TRIM
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.

FET COMPRESSOR / RMS LEVELLER

The module is titled for the model in use.

GAIN REDUCTION
An edgewise meter: the needle rests at 0 on the right and swings left as it compresses, to -20 dB.
INPUT
Drives the signal into the fixed threshold, 0 - 36 dB (12 dB). On CLEAN it sets the leveller's threshold in the same way. Level matched: more INPUT means more compression, not more level.
ATTACK
OB: 20 - 800 microseconds (250). On CLEAN the same knob sets the leveller's attack, 37.5 times longer (0.75 - 30 ms).
RELEASE
50 ms - 1.1 s (150 ms), programme dependent on both models: short peaks recover in the set time; after sustained gain reduction the last of it lets go more slowly.
RATIO
4, 8, 12 or 20 : 1 (4), or ALL. On OB the FET's knee is soft and curved. ALL is the all-buttons sound: about 40:1 just over the threshold bending over to about 12:1, a lazier attack, a detector that digs in after every hit and lets go (pumping), and more distortion. On CLEAN the knee is 12 dB wide and ALL is a plain 20:1.

SIDE-CHAIN

HIGH-PASS
OFF, 60, 120 or 250 Hz, 12 dB per octave, in the detector only (yellow keys, the house colour for a high-pass): the lows stop pulling the gain down. The change fades out and back in.
DETECTOR
The notes show PEAK (OB) or RMS (CLEAN). The two channels share one detector (linked stereo).

OUTPUT

WANDER
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on OB the FET's bias wanders (the threshold, the release timing, and the two channels track the gain reduction slightly differently); on CLEAN only a trace remains.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.
MIX
A fader with a blue cap: dry to compressed, 0 - 100% (100%), for parallel compression. The dry signal is delayed to match.
OUTPUT
A fader: the output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).

Two models

Every Series 74 unit has two models, chosen with the OB / CLEAN lenses in the header. OB is the heavy mobile field sound: transformers, saturation and colour. CLEAN is the high-headroom, transparent broadcast path. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel changes with the model: OB is an olive-drab flight case with gunmetal knobs and amber lenses; CLEAN is a broadcast desk, with light-grey modules, red-capped aluminium knobs, cream keys that light red and cream VU windows.

OB
The field compressor: a FET peak compressor with a feedback detector, microsecond attack and ALL buttons. The FET's distortion grows as it works, and a class-A stage and an output transformer add colour. It grabs and pumps.
CLEAN
A transparent RMS leveller: an RMS detector, a 12 dB soft knee and a programme-dependent release. No added harmonics; the class-A stage and the transformer are out of the path.

Presets

A preset sets INPUT, ATTACK, RELEASE, RATIO, the side-chain high-pass, MIX, OUTPUT and the model; TRIM, GUARD, WANDER and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Init
OB, 12 dB of INPUT at 4:1.
Vocal Grab
OB at 4:1, driven a little harder, with the 60 Hz side-chain filter: a lead vocal held up front with FET bite.
Vocal Smooth
CLEAN at 4:1 with a slower release and the 120 Hz filter: transparent levelling for a vocal.
De-Harsh Vocal
OB at 4:1 with a fast attack, a quick release and the 250 Hz side-chain filter, so the detector hears the upper mids and catches hard, edgy peaks.
Bass Even
OB at 8:1 with a slower attack and a long release, holding a bass steady.
Kick Punch
OB at 4:1 with a slow attack and a fast release, so the kick's click comes through.
Snare Crack
OB at 8:1 driven hard, with a slow attack and a fast release: the crack comes through and the body is pulled up behind it.
Drum Bus Punch
OB at 4:1 with a slow attack, a fast release and the 120 Hz filter, for a drum bus.
Parallel Smash
ALL driven hard, mixed in at 40% under the dry signal.
Room Mics
OB with ALL buttons in and a fast release: pumping room and ambient mics.
Acoustic Guitar
CLEAN at 4:1 with the 120 Hz filter: even strumming without colour.
Mix Bus Glue
CLEAN at 4:1, light INPUT, a slow attack and the 120 Hz filter: a few dB of glue on the mix bus.
Reset all to defaults
(Last in the list.) Every control back to its starting value.

Tips

  • Switch between OB and CLEAN at the same INPUT to hear what the FET, the class-A stage and the transformer add: the level stays the same.
  • For the classic all-buttons room sound, set ALL, a fast release and plenty of INPUT, then bring it in under the dry signal with MIX.
  • A slower ATTACK lets the front of a drum or a consonant through before the FET grabs it.
  • If the bass makes a mix pump, set the SIDE-CHAIN high-pass to 120 or 250 Hz.
