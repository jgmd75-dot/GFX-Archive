Dynamics

Fet92

Series 92's studio compressor: a boutique opto cell with a valve output, or the precise dynamics of an early digital processor, on one switch.

Overview

Fet92 is the main compressor of the Series 92 home studio: high-end home recording from the 90s, the rack outboard and DSP boxes of a serious home studio of the decade. It has two models on one switch.

ANALOG is the affordable boutique opto compressor of the late 90s with a valve output. A lamp lights a light-dependent cell: a fast attack that speeds up on big jumps, and a two-stage, programme-dependent release - the first part of the gain comes back in the RELEASE time, the rest lingers for seconds when the cell has been held down. ATTACK and RELEASE bias the cell around its natural timing. The valve make-up stage adds warmth that rises with the gain reduction. DIGITAL is the dynamics section of an early digital processor: a precise feed-forward compressor with an RMS or PEAK detector, a HARD or SOFT knee and exact attack and release times. It adds nothing of its own and hands on a clean 20-bit word.

COMPRESS drives the signal into a fixed threshold. Every COMPRESS and RATIO setting is level matched, and the two models are matched to each other, so COMPRESS changes the amount of compression rather than the loudness.

Reach for it for

  • Smooth, warm levelling of vocals, bass and acoustic instruments
  • Opto glue on a drum bus or a whole mix
  • Precise, transparent control with exact times
  • Parallel compression with the MIX fader

Getting started

  1. Insert Fet92 on a track. It opens on Init: ANALOG, 12 dB of COMPRESS, 4:1.
  2. Turn COMPRESS for more gain reduction; the level stays put. Watch the GR ladder.
  3. Set ATTACK and RELEASE; on ANALOG they nudge the opto cell, on DIGITAL they are exact.
  4. Switch to DIGITAL for a clean compressor; pick RMS or PEAK and a HARD or SOFT knee.
  5. Blend with the MIX fader and set the level with the OUTPUT fader.

Controls

The header

PRESETS
Opens the preset list (a click on the preset display does the same). The list ends with Reset all to defaults.
◄ / ►
Step to the previous or next preset.
Preset display
Shows the name of the preset in use. New instances open on Init.
ANALOG / DIGITAL
The model: the character of the unit (see Two models). Presets may set it.
S / M / L
Panel size: small, medium or large. The panel also scales to fit the plugin window.
?
Opens a pop-up with the design credit, the GFX website address and the address of this manual.

INPUT

IN / OUT ladders
LED ladders, -30 to +6 (0 = 0 VU): the level going into the compressor and coming out.
TRIM
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.

OPTO · VALVE / DYNAMICS

The section follows the model. Its LCD shows the cell and the ratio (ANALOG), or the threshold the INPUT knob has set and the ratio (DIGITAL).

GR ladder
An LED ladder of the gain reduction, 0 to -20 dB, lit in the lamp colour.
COMPRESS / INPUT
How hard the signal is driven into the fixed threshold, 0 - 36 dB (12 dB). Level matched: it sets the amount of compression, not the loudness. Called INPUT on DIGITAL.
ATTACK
0.1 - 100 ms (10 ms). ANALOG: biases the opto cell around its natural 10 ms (about 0.6 to 40 ms over the range); the cell still answers big jumps faster. DIGITAL: the exact attack time.
RELEASE
20 - 2000 ms (300 ms). ANALOG: biases the cell's first release stage around its natural 300 ms (about 45 ms to 1.1 s); after a long stretch of gain reduction the last part lingers for seconds. DIGITAL: the exact release time.
RATIO
Keys: 2, 4 (default), 8 or LIMIT. ANALOG: the cell's wide, soft knee (narrower as the ratio rises); LIMIT is 20:1. DIGITAL: LIMIT is infinity : 1. RATIO glides, so it can be changed while playing.

SIDE-CHAIN

HIGH-PASS
Yellow keys (the house high-pass colour): OFF, 60, 120 or 250 Hz, 12 dB per octave, in the side-chain only: the lows stop pulling the gain down. The change fades out and back in.
KNEE
DIGITAL only: HARD, or SOFT (a 10 dB knee, default). The change glides. Unlit on ANALOG, where the cell has its own soft knee.
DETECTOR
DIGITAL only: RMS (a 10 ms window, default) or PEAK. The change fades out and back in. Unlit on ANALOG. The two channels share one detector (linked).

OUTPUT

DRIFT
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on ANALOG the valve warms up and the mains wander, so the threshold, the cell's release and the valve's bias drift slowly and the channels track the gain reduction slightly differently; on DIGITAL only a trace remains.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.
MIX
A fader with a blue cap: dry to compressed, 0 - 100% (100%). The dry signal is delayed to match.
OUTPUT
A fader: the output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).

Two models

Every Series 92 unit has two models, chosen with the ANALOG / DIGITAL keys in the header - the two halves of a serious 90s home studio. ANALOG is the boutique outboard: valve, opto and transformer colour. DIGITAL is the first affordable 16 / 20-bit converters and DSP: clean, precise and a little glassy. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel lamps glow amber on ANALOG and green on DIGITAL.

ANALOG
A boutique opto compressor with a valve output: a fast cell with a lingering, programme-dependent release, a soft knee, and valve warmth that rises with the gain reduction.
DIGITAL
The dynamics section of an early digital processor: feed-forward, RMS or PEAK, HARD or SOFT knee, exact attack and release, no colour, a clean 20-bit word. The KNEE and DETECTOR keys work only here.

Presets

A preset sets COMPRESS, ATTACK, RELEASE, RATIO, the side-chain high-pass, MIX, OUTPUT, KNEE, DETECTOR and the model; TRIM, GUARD, DRIFT and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Init
ANALOG, 12 dB of COMPRESS, 4:1, the cell at its natural timing.
Opto Vocal

Bass DI Squeeze
ANALOG at 8:1, a slower attack to keep the pluck.
Acoustic Strum
ANALOG at 2:1, gentle, the 120 Hz side-chain filter.
Drum Bus Glue
ANALOG at 2:1 with a 30 ms attack and a quick release.
Parallel Smash
ANALOG LIMIT driven hard and fast, mixed in at 40%.
Valve Limiter
ANALOG LIMIT with a fast attack.
Mix Bus Warmth
ANALOG at 2:1, a light touch with a slow release.
Digital Vocal Rider
DIGITAL RMS, soft knee, 4:1.
Peak Limit
DIGITAL PEAK, hard knee, LIMIT, 0.1 ms attack.
Clean Bass Leveller
DIGITAL RMS at 8:1 with a 20 ms attack.
DAT Master Bus
DIGITAL RMS, soft knee, 2:1, slow times and the 60 Hz side-chain filter.
Reset all to defaults
(Last in the list.) Every control back to its starting value.

Tips

  • Switch between ANALOG and DIGITAL at the same settings to hear opto and valve against clean digital: the level stays the same.
  • On ANALOG, hold a long phrase down and then stop: the last few dB come back slowly. Short peaks recover in the RELEASE time.
  • Raise the side-chain HIGH-PASS when a bass or kick pumps the whole mix.
  • Parallel Smash shows MIX at work: keep the dry signal and add a crushed one under it.
