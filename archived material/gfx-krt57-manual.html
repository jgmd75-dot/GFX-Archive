Mastering

KRT57

Series 57's enhancer: a delay equaliser holds the lows back, valve generators restore the bass and the presence, a gentle brilliance on top.

Overview

KRT57 is the Series 57 counterpart of KRT81, the phase and frequency corrector. The idea is the same: hold the lows back a little so the edge arrives first, and restore what the chain has dulled. It works with the means of the valve broadcast house.

ALIGN is the line's delay equaliser: two all-pass sections that change the timing of the lows without changing any level. BASS is a valve harmonic generator on the lows: it adds the bass's harmonics, so the bass is heard more clearly on small speakers without more low-frequency energy. PRESENCE is a valve exciter on the top: the band above FREQ is driven and only the new harmonics are added back. The generators work at a steady level whatever comes in, as the exciters did with a limiter ahead of the valve, and run oversampled 2x.

Reach for it for

  • Clarity and lift on a dull mix or recording
  • Bass that reads on small speakers
  • Presence on vocals without harsh EQ
  • Restoring the top of old tape or samples
  • Tighter transients on a bus (ALIGN)

Getting started

  1. Insert KRT57 on a track, bus or master. It opens on Init: a gentle lift.
  2. Raise PRESENCE AMOUNT for clarity; move FREQ down for more body in the effect, up for air.
  3. Raise BASS AMOUNT and set FREQ near the bass's fundamental.
  4. Try ALIGN on drums and mixes: the attack comes forward.
  5. Use the IN button to compare with the untouched sound.

Two faces

Every Series 57 unit has two faces, chosen with the ULM / COPENHAGEN keys in the header. Each changes the look and the sound, and the two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. Presets leave the face as it is.

ULM
A crisp exciter, even and odd harmonics in equal parts; a tight bass generator; the presence band as set.
COPENHAGEN
Warmer, mostly even harmonics; the presence band a little lower; a softer brilliance; a rounder bass.

Presets

A preset sets DRIVE, ALIGN, BASS, PRESENCE, BRILLIANCE and MIX, and switches the unit in; the face, INPUT, GUARD, OUTPUT, DRIFT and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Tips

  • A little goes a long way: on a master, keep both AMOUNTs under 30%.
  • Bass harmonics make the bass audible on small speakers without adding energy that eats headroom.
  • ALIGN changes no levels; listen to the attack and the depth.
  • COPENHAGEN's even harmonics flatter voices; ULM's crisper mix suits drums and guitars.
