KRT74

Series 74's OB maximizer: a punchy low-end enhancer with iron and bass harmonics, or a surgical phase and spectral corrector, on one switch.

Overview

KRT74 is the Series 74 counterpart of KRT57: the low-end maximizer from the outside-broadcast truck, with two models on one switch.

OB is a psychoacoustic low-end enhancer. The lows below TUNE are split off: PUNCH lifts the attack of each kick and bass note, IRON runs the band through a saturating transformer core, and LOW AMOUNT adds the bass's own 2nd and 3rd harmonics just above TUNE, so the low end reads on small speakers without more low-frequency energy. HIGH is a presence exciter on the top, and DRIVE an input transformer. CLEAN is a surgical phase and spectral corrector with no added harmonics: an all-pass phase rotation below TUNE to tighten the lows, a low shelf, a dynamic evener that pulls back low-mid build-up only when it happens, and a high tilt.

The processing runs at 2x oversampling. DRIVE is level matched, and the two models are matched to each other at the defaults, so the switch changes the character, not the loudness. Changing the model fades the sound out and back in; the latency is 31 samples, reported to REAPER.

Reach for it for

  • Bass and kick that disappear on phones and small speakers
  • Punch and weight on drum and bass buses
  • Tightening a boomy low end without EQ boosts
  • Taming low-mid build-up on a mix bus

Getting started

  1. Insert KRT74 on a track or bus. It opens on Init: OB, LOW AMOUNT at 50%, TUNE at 80 Hz.
  2. Set TUNE to the bottom of the bass or kick, then turn LOW AMOUNT until the low end reads on small speakers.
  3. Add PUNCH for attack and IRON for thickness; HIGH brings the top forward.
  4. Switch to CLEAN to correct rather than enhance: ROTATE tightens the lows, LOW-MID evens out the build-up.
  5. Blend with MIX and set the level with OUTPUT.

LOW · BASS ENHANCER / LOW · PHASE CORRECTOR

The title follows the model, and so do the labels: on CLEAN the knobs take their CLEAN names.

The colour code
LOW AMOUNT and TUNE have brown caps (the low band) and HIGH a red cap (the top); on CLEAN, LOW-MID has a blue cap (the low mids).
LOW AMOUNT / LOW LEVEL
OB: how much of the bass's 2nd and 3rd harmonics is added, 0 - 100% (50%). The harmonics are generated at a steady level, so the colour is the same loud or quiet. CLEAN: a low shelf at TUNE, -6 to +6 dB (50% is flat).
TUNE
Where the low band ends, 40 - 160 Hz (80 Hz). On OB the band that is enhanced; on CLEAN the shelf's corner and the top of the phase rotation.
PUNCH / ROTATE
OB: a transient shaper on the low band, 0 - 100% (30%): the attack of each low note is lifted. CLEAN: the phase rotation, 0 - 100% (30%): two all-pass sections hold the lows back, lowering the crest factor and tightening the low end. The level of every frequency stays the same; at 0 there is none.
IRON / LOW-MID
OB: how hard the low band drives the transformer core, 0 - 100% (40%): thicker, rounder lows. CLEAN: the dynamic evener, 0 - 100% (40%): when the low mids (about three times TUNE) build up against the rest of the spectrum, a gentle dip pulls them back, up to about -6 dB.
The read-out
OB: bars showing the 2nd and 3rd harmonics being added. CLEAN: the depth of the low-mid evener's dip as it works.

HIGH · DRIVE

DRIVE is OB only: on CLEAN it is out of the path and its label greys.

HIGH / TILT
OB: the presence exciter, 0 - 100% (50%): the top above about 3 kHz is driven and only the new harmonics are added back. CLEAN: a high shelf at 4.5 kHz, -4 to +4 dB (50% is flat).
DRIVE
The input transformer, 0 - 100% (20%). Level matched: it adds colour, not loudness.

OUTPUT

MIX and OUTPUT are desk faders: drag the cap, SHIFT for fine moves, double-click for the default.

MIX
The blue fader: dry to processed, 0 - 100% (100%). The dry signal is delayed to match.
OUTPUT
The cream fader: the output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
WANDER
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on OB the iron's bias and the gain wander slowly and the channels never quite match; on CLEAN only a trace of gain wander remains.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.

Two models

Every Series 74 unit has two models, chosen with the OB / CLEAN lenses in the header. OB is the heavy mobile field sound: transformers, saturation and colour. CLEAN is the high-headroom, transparent broadcast path. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel changes with the model: OB is an olive-drab flight case with gunmetal knobs and amber lenses; CLEAN is a broadcast desk, with light-grey modules, red-capped aluminium knobs, cream keys that light red and cream VU windows.

OB
A punchy psychoacoustic low-end enhancer: an input transformer, a transient shaper and transformer iron on the low band, the bass's own harmonics added above TUNE, and a presence exciter on the top.
CLEAN
A surgical phase and spectral corrector with no added harmonics: an all-pass phase rotation below TUNE, a low shelf, a dynamic low-mid evener and a high tilt. DRIVE is out of the path.

Presets

A preset sets DRIVE, LOW AMOUNT, TUNE, PUNCH, IRON, HIGH, MIX, OUTPUT and the model; TRIM, GUARD, WANDER and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Tips

  • Check OB on small speakers or a phone: LOW AMOUNT is about what you hear there, not about the meter.
  • Set TUNE just above the lowest note that matters: too high and the harmonics start to colour the low mids.
  • On CLEAN, ROTATE lowers the peaks of the low end without changing its tone, which leaves more room before the limiter.
  • LOW-MID only works when the low mids build up, so it can stay on a mix bus and leave the quiet parts alone.
