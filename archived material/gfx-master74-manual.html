Mastering

Master74

Series 74's 2-bus mastering suite: a glue-heavy VCA bus compressor and a soft clipper, or a transparent leveller and a true-peak limiter, on one switch.

Overview

Master74 is the Series 74 mastering suite: the programme chain at the end of an outside-broadcast truck, between the mix bus and the line out. The signal runs through EQ, a BUS COMPRESSOR, STEREO width and mono bass, and a LIMITER, with meters all the way.

OB is glue-heavy: a VCA bus compressor with a firm knee, an input transformer and the console bus's summing amp (IRON), passive-style EQ curves, and a fast limiter into a soft clipper at 2x. CLEAN is transparent mastering: the compressor becomes a gentle leveller, the EQ and the path are clean, and the limiter is a lookahead true-peak limiter with 4x peak detection and a ceiling in dBTP.

The two models are level matched at the default settings. The latency is the same in both, and with the limiter out (2 ms plus 11 samples, 107 samples at 48 kHz, reported to REAPER).

Reach for it for

  • Gluing and finishing a stereo mix or a stem bus
  • Safe broadcast and streaming peaks with a true-peak ceiling
  • Mono bass and width control on the master
  • VCA bus compression with console colour

Getting started

  1. Insert Master74 on the master bus or a mix bus. It opens on Init: OB, 4:1, a ceiling of -1 dB.
  2. Set THRESHOLD until the compressor's GR meter moves 1 - 3 dB on the loud parts; add MAKEUP to taste.
  3. Shape the tone with LOW, MID and HIGH, and use MONO BASS to keep the low end centred.
  4. Turn up DRIVE into the limiter for loudness, watching its GR meter and the LUFS read-out.
  5. Switch to CLEAN for a transparent master with a true-peak ceiling.

INPUT

IN meter
An edgewise meter, lit amber on OB and orange-red on CLEAN: the level coming in.
TRIM
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works. On a hot master you may want it off.

EQ

The ON / OFF keys in the header switch the EQ in or out (the change fades out and back in). The title reads COLOURED on OB and CLEAN on CLEAN. Each band's gain and FREQ knobs share its colour: LOW brown, MID green, HIGH red.

LOW
A low shelf, -6 to +6 dB (0 dB). Its FREQ knob below sets the corner, 30 - 300 Hz (80 Hz).
MID
A bell, -6 to +6 dB (0 dB). FREQ 200 Hz - 8 kHz (1.5 kHz). Broad on OB, a little tighter on CLEAN.
HIGH
A high shelf, -6 to +6 dB (0 dB). FREQ · AIR 2 - 20 kHz (10 kHz): above about 14 kHz it is an air band.
OB / CLEAN
On OB the shelves are passive-style, with a little overshoot at the corner; on CLEAN they are flat Butterworth shelves. A band at 0 dB is out of the path.

BUS COMPRESSOR

The ON / OFF keys in the header switch the compressor in or out. The title reads VCA on OB and LEVELLER on CLEAN.

GR meter
The gain reduction: the needle rests at 0 on the right and swings left as it compresses.
THRESHOLD
-30 to 0 dB (-10 dB).
MAKEUP
Gain after the compressor, 0 - 12 dB (0 dB).
RATIO
2:1, 4:1 or 10:1 (4:1). On CLEAN the leveller uses half the ratio's slope, with a 12 dB knee.
ATTACK
0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, 10 or 30 ms (10 ms). Slower on CLEAN.
RELEASE
0.1, 0.3, 0.6 or 1.2 s, or AUTO (AUTO): programme dependent, quick after short peaks and slower when it works for longer. Longer on CLEAN.
SC HPF
The side-chain high-pass, on yellow keys (the house colour for a high-pass): OFF, 60, 90 or 150 Hz (OFF). The compressor then reacts less to the bass.
OB / CLEAN
On OB a VCA bus compressor: peak sensing, a firm 2 dB knee, followed by the console bus. On CLEAN a gentle, transparent leveller: RMS sensing, a wide knee, slower times.

STEREO

WIDTH
The side signal, 0 - 200% (100%): MONO at 0%, wider above 100%.
MONO BASS
Yellow keys: OFF, 60, 100 or 150 Hz (OFF). Below it the sides are taken out, so the low end is mono. The change fades out and back in.

LIMITER

The ON / OFF keys in the header switch the limiter (and DRIVE) in or out. The title reads SOFT CLIP on OB and TRUE PEAK on CLEAN; the small print beside the meter says how it works and shows the latency in samples.

GR meter
The limiter's gain reduction (on OB including what the clipper takes off).
DRIVE
A fader with a yellow cap: gain into the limiter, 0 - 12 dB (0 dB).
CEILING
The most it lets out, -12 to 0 dB (-1 dB): dBFS (sample peak) on OB, dBTP (true peak) on CLEAN.
RELEASE
10 - 1000 ms (100 ms). Programme dependent: it slows when the limiter works for a while. Smoother on CLEAN.
OB / CLEAN
On OB a fast limiter with no lookahead, into a soft clipper at 2x that rounds the transients it lets through. On CLEAN a lookahead limiter: the true peak is found at 4x, the gain is down before the peak arrives (2 ms lookahead) and lets go smoothly.

OUTPUT

OUT meter
The level going out.
SHORT-TERM LUFS
The loudness of the last 3 seconds (K-weighted) in a nixie read-out, with the output's PEAK in dBFS below it.
OUTPUT
A fader: the output level after the limiter, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB). Above 0 dB it lifts the peaks over the CEILING.
IRON
OB only (its label greys on CLEAN), 0 - 100% (40%): how hard the input transformer and the console bus work. The lows saturate first, and the summing amp adds even harmonics.
WANDER
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on OB the compressor's threshold and the console's drive drift a little; on CLEAN only a trace of threshold wander remains.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak. On CLEAN with the limiter in, the clip is lifted clear of the CEILING, so the limiter has the last word.

Two models

Every Series 74 unit has two models, chosen with the OB / CLEAN lenses in the header. OB is the heavy mobile field sound: transformers, saturation and colour. CLEAN is the high-headroom, transparent broadcast path. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel changes with the model: OB is an olive-drab flight case with gunmetal knobs and amber lenses; CLEAN is a broadcast desk, with light-grey modules, red-capped aluminium knobs, cream keys that light red and cream VU windows.

OB
Glue and colour: a VCA bus compressor with a firm knee, the input iron and the console bus, passive-style EQ curves, and a fast limiter into a soft clipper at 2x. The ceiling is a sample peak.
CLEAN
Transparent mastering: a gentle RMS leveller, clean EQ, no iron, and a lookahead true-peak limiter (4x detection, 2 ms lookahead, smooth release). The ceiling is in dBTP and holds before the house clip.

Presets

A preset sets the EQ, the compressor, STEREO, the limiter, OUTPUT, IRON and the model; TRIM, GUARD, WANDER and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness, so their OUTPUT sits a little below 0 dB: raise it (or DRIVE) to bring the peaks up to the ceiling.

Init
OB, 4:1 at -10 dB, auto release, a ceiling of -1 dB.

Stadium Wide

Live Music Relay

Commentary Mix

Punch Bus

Crush Feed

Transmission Limit

Clean Master

Clean Broadcast
CLEAN, firmer levelling and 4 dB into the true-peak limiter.

Tips

  • Switch between OB and CLEAN to hear what the glue and the iron add: at the default settings the level stays the same.
  • Set SC HPF to 60 or 90 Hz on bass-heavy mixes, so the kick does not pump the whole mix.
  • For streaming, use CLEAN with the CEILING at -1 dB and watch the LUFS read-out while you set DRIVE.
  • Keep OUTPUT at 0 dB or below after the limiter: above it, the peaks go over the CEILING.
