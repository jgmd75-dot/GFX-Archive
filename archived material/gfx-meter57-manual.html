Mastering

Meter57

Series 57's meter: two moving-coil meters (VU, PPM or peak), a cathode-ray stereo scope, a magic-eye correlation tube and nixie peak read-outs. Bit-exact.

Overview

Meter57 is the Series 57 counterpart of Meter81: the measuring corner of a late-50s control room. Two big moving-coil meters sit in amber windows, a small cathode-ray tube draws the stereo picture (the stereo disc was new in 1957), a magic-eye tube shows the correlation, and the peaks are held on nixie tubes.

The audio is not touched: the plug-in only reads the signal, so its output is bit-exact with its input and the latency is 0.

Reach for it for

  • Gain staging to a reference level (VU)
  • Broadcast-style peak programme metering (PPM)
  • Checking peaks before the master
  • Checking stereo width and phase

Getting started

  1. Insert Meter57 anywhere: it does not change the sound.
  2. Pick the SCALE: VU for average level, PPM for programme peaks, PEAK for sample peaks.
  3. Set REF to your alignment level (0 VU / 0 dB = REF dBFS).
  4. Watch the scope and the magic eye for the stereo picture; click the nixie tubes to reset the peaks.

The meters

VU
The volume indicator: the rectified average drives a modelled needle (300 ms to 99%, about 1.5% overshoot). -20 to +3 VU; 0 VU = REF.
PPM
The quasi-peak programme meter: a 5 ms integration (a 10 ms burst reads 1 dB under) and a slow, steady return. The face picks the standard: ULM the German DIN scale (-50 to +5 dB, a return of 20 dB in 1.5 s), COPENHAGEN the Nordic scale (-36 to +9 dB, 20 dB in 1.7 s). 0 dB = REF.
PEAK
The sample peak: instant attack, a return of 20 dB in 1.7 s. -60 to 0 dBFS; REF does not apply.
REF
The alignment level: 0 VU / 0 dB = -18, -14, -12 or -9 dBFS. Sine-referenced: a sine whose peak is at REF reads 0.
HOLD
OFF, 2 S or INF: an orange drag pointer on each meter.
PEAK L / R
Nixie tubes: the true sample peak of each channel since the last reset, in dBFS. Click them to reset; with HOLD OFF they hold each peak for 2 s.
OVER
Lights at 0 dBFS and above.

STEREO

The scope
The stereo picture, mid up and side across: a vertical line is mono, a circle is wide, a horizontal line is out of phase. Green phosphor in ULM, amber in COPENHAGEN.
CORRELATION
The magic eye closes as the channels agree: fully closed = +1 (mono), half open = 0 (unrelated), wide open with the shadow red = negative (out of phase). The nixie shows the number.

Two faces

Every Series 57 unit has two faces, chosen with the ULM / COPENHAGEN keys in the header. Each changes the look and the sound, and the two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. Presets leave the face as it is.

ULM
The alabaster look; the PPM follows the German DIN standard; the scope's phosphor is green.
COPENHAGEN
The Danish look; the PPM follows the Nordic standard; the scope's phosphor is amber.

Tips

  • There is no DRIFT on Meter57: a meter must not wander.
  • For tracking, aim the VU around 0 at REF -18: peaks then sit well clear of 0 dBFS.
  • A correlation that drops below 0 for long is worth checking in mono.
  • The PPM standard follows the face, so switching to COPENHAGEN is also switching to the Nordic scale.
