Mastering

Meter74

Series 74's metering bay: twin black-faced peak programme meters, or a laboratory reference meter with true peak and loudness. Bit-exact.

Overview

Meter74 is the metering bay of an outside-broadcast truck, with two faceplates on one switch. The audio is not touched: the output is bit-exact with the input and the latency is 0, unless you switch in the line transformer.

OB shows twin peak programme meters in the British broadcast style: black faces, white marks 1 to 7, and PPM 4 at the alignment level. They have the ballistics of the IEC Type IIa meter: a 10 ms integration and a slow, steady fall of 24 dB in 2.8 s. The needles read left and right, or M and S. A LINE TRANSFORMER can be switched into the path for a touch of iron. CLEAN is an uncoloured laboratory reference meter, its windows lit orange-red: true-peak bars (4x oversampled) with the RMS beside them, the loudness per ITU-R BS.1770 / EBU R128 (momentary, short-term and integrated), held true-peak maxima and a correlation meter.

The meters always run in the background, so the integrated loudness keeps counting while the OB face is showing.

Reach for it for

  • Broadcast-style peak metering to an alignment level
  • Checking loudness against a target in LUFS
  • Catching true peaks before the master
  • Checking the stereo picture in M and S, and the correlation

Getting started

  1. Insert Meter74 anywhere: on OB with the line transformer off, it does not change the sound.
  2. Set ALIGN to your alignment level: PPM 4 = -18, -14 or -12 dBFS.
  3. Choose L / R or M / S for the needles.
  4. Switch to CLEAN to read true peak, RMS, loudness and correlation; press RESET to start a new integrated reading.
  5. For a touch of iron, switch the LINE TRANSFORMER on (OB only): the output is then no longer bit-exact.

PEAK PROGRAMME METERS

The OB face.

The meters
Two black-faced peak programme meters, marks 1 to 7: 6 dB between 1 and 2, then 4 dB per mark, and PPM 4 = the ALIGN level (a sine whose peak sits at that level reads 4). A 10 ms integration (a 10 ms burst reads 2 dB under the steady tone) and a fall of 24 dB in 2.8 s. The letter and the coloured dot on each face show what it reads: L red, R green, M white, S yellow.
OVER
Lights for a second when a sample reaches 0 dBFS.

TRUE PEAK · RMS

The CLEAN face.

The bars
One bar per channel in a window lit orange-red: the true peak (4x oversampled, dBTP) on the wide charcoal bar, dark red above 0 dBTP, with a fall of 20 dB in 1.7 s and a cream line holding the highest peak for 2 s. The narrow bar beside it is the RMS (300 ms). The scale runs from -60 to +3, with the top 30 dB opened out.
RMS
The RMS of each channel in dBFS, under its bar. A full-scale sine reads -3 dB.
TP MAX
The highest true peak of each channel since the last reset, in dBTP, in lit orange-red read-outs. Click the read-outs to reset them. The red LED between them is OVER.

LOUDNESS · BS.1770

The CLEAN face, in read-outs lit orange-red. K-weighted, per ITU-R BS.1770 and EBU R128.

MOMENTARY
The loudness over the last 400 ms, in LUFS.
SHORT-TERM
The loudness over the last 3 s, in LUFS.
INTEGRATED
The loudness since the last reset, gated: blocks below -70 LUFS are left out, then blocks more than 10 LU below the average.
RESET
Clears the integrated reading.
CORRELATION
A charcoal bar from the centre, in a window lit orange-red: +1 is mono, 0 is wide or unrelated, and below 0 (red) is out of phase. The number is shown on the right.

ALIGN · LINE

ALIGN
The alignment level for the PPMs: PPM 4 = -18, -14 or -12 dBFS (-18). On CLEAN it is greyed: it sets the OB face only.
NEEDLES
L / R reads left and right; M / S reads the mid (L + R) and the side (L - R), each 3 dB down, so a centred mono source reads about 3 dB higher on M than on L and R, and nothing on S (L / R). Greyed on CLEAN.
LINE TRANSFORMER
OFF or ON (OFF). ON passes the audio through a line transformer with gentle iron: the lows round off a little when driven hard, with a trace of high-frequency loss and a coupling capacitor at 4 Hz. The output is then no longer bit-exact, and the panel says so. Switching crossfades over 20 ms, so there is no click and no gap. On CLEAN it is forced out.

Two models

Every Series 74 unit has two models, chosen with the OB / CLEAN lenses in the header. OB is the heavy mobile field sound: transformers, saturation and colour. CLEAN is the high-headroom, transparent broadcast path. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel changes with the model: OB is an olive-drab flight case with gunmetal knobs and amber lenses; CLEAN is a broadcast desk, with light-grey modules, red-capped aluminium knobs, cream keys that light red and cream VU windows.

OB
Twin black-faced peak programme meters in the British broadcast style, with ALIGN, L / R or M / S needles and the optional line transformer.
CLEAN
An uncoloured laboratory reference meter on the light-grey broadcast-desk plates, its windows lit orange-red: true peak, RMS, BS.1770 loudness and correlation. The line transformer is forced out, so the audio is always bit-exact.

Presets

A preset sets the model, ALIGN, the line transformer and the needles. There are six presets, because Meter74 has only four switches. A meter has no loudness to level.

Init
OB, PPM 4 = -18 dBFS, L / R, the transformer off.
Stereo M/S
OB with the needles on M and S.
Align -14
OB with PPM 4 = -14 dBFS.
Hot Desk -12
OB with PPM 4 = -12 dBFS.
Line Transformer
OB with the line transformer switched in.
Lab Reference
The CLEAN face.
Reset all to defaults
(Last in the list.) Every control back to its starting value.

Tips

  • There is no WANDER on Meter74: a meter must not wander.
  • At ALIGN -18, line-up tone reads PPM 4, and normal programme peaks should reach about PPM 6.
  • For speech or music, -23 LUFS integrated is the EBU R128 target; press RESET at the start of the programme.
  • Watch the S needle on M / S: if it reads as high as M, the stereo is very wide or out of phase. Check the correlation on CLEAN.
