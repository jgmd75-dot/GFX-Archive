Mastering

Meter81

A stereo level meter with PPM, VU and peak scales, peak hold, a phase correlation meter and two modes.

Overview

Meter81 is a stereo level meter in the spirit of the 1980s and 90s broadcast and studio test gear: glowing vacuum-fluorescent bargraphs or chunky LED ladders. It only listens. The audio passes through bit for bit unchanged, with no latency, so you can leave it on any track or on the master without it touching the sound.

It has three scales. PPM is a quasi-peak programme meter of the broadcast kind: it rises fast and falls slowly, so it shows how loud the peaks really feel. VU is the classic volume indicator, with the ballistics of a weighted needle, that shows the average level, the way the ear hears loudness. PEAK shows the sample peaks in dBFS. Below the meters a KORREL. meter shows the phase correlation between left and right, so you can see at a glance whether a mix is safe in mono.

All readings are referenced to a sine: a sine whose peak sits at the REF level reads 0 dB (PPM) or 0 VU. There is no DRIFT and no output stage: a meter has nothing to colour.

Reach for it for

  • Lining up levels for broadcast and mastering
  • Gain staging tracks and buses on a VU scale
  • Watching sample peaks and overs on the master
  • Checking mono compatibility with the correlation meter
  • A good-looking meter to keep open while you mix

Two modes

Meter81 has one MODE switch, the VFD / MATRIX keys in the header. The mode changes the look, the labels and the sound together. VFD stands for vacuum fluorescent display: the glowing cyan and green segment readouts of 80s Japanese equipment. VFD is the Japanese module: a charcoal panel with glowing readouts and English labels. MATRIX is the European module: a battleship-grey panel with LED-matrix readouts and English labels. VU, PEAK, REF, HOLD, the readout and the correlation meter work the same in both modes. Both PPM followers run all the time, so switching the mode changes the reading at once; nothing touches the audio.

VFD
A charcoal panel with cyan vacuum-fluorescent bargraph segments (the unlit segments glow faintly) and English labels. On the PPM scale it uses fast peak programme ballistics: 1 ms integration and a slow fall of 20 dB in 2.5 s.
MATRIX
A battleship-grey panel with stacked LED blocks, green to amber to red, and English labels. On the PPM scale it uses DIN 45406 quasi-peak ballistics: 10 ms integration and a return of 20 dB in 1.5 s.

Meters

Two meters, L and R, drawn as two horizontal bargraphs with the scale between them. Each shows the scale name and its alignment (for example 0 dB = -9 dBFS), and the footer prints the ballistics in use (INTEGR. and the fall time). Click a meter to clear the holds.

PPM scale
-50 to +5 dB, 0 dB = the REF level. MATRIX: a 10 ms tone burst reads 1 dB under the steady reading, and the meter falls back 20 dB in 1.5 s. VFD: a 1 ms burst reads 1 dB under, and the fall is a slow 20 dB in 2.5 s.
VU scale
-20 to +3 VU, 0 VU = the REF level. The reading follows a modelled needle with mass and damping: it reaches its reading in about 300 ms with a tiny overshoot, and falls back just as smoothly.
PEAK scale
-60 to 0 dBFS, sample peak, instant rise and a smooth fall of 20 dB in 1.7 s. REF does not apply.
Hold marker
With HOLD on, one bright segment marks the highest reading, then falls back onto the meter.

KORREL. (PHASE)

Correlation meter
-1 to +1, averaged over about 200 ms. +1 is mono (L and R the same), 0 is wide or unrelated, and anything below 0 (the orange half) means the channels are out of phase and will lose level or tone when summed to mono.

PEAK dBFS readout

L / R
The true sample peak of each channel in dBFS, held for the HOLD time (with HOLD OFF the numbers still hold for 1 s so you can read them). Click the readout to clear it.
OVER
Two lamps that light when a channel reaches 0 dBFS or above.

SCALE

PPM / VU / PEAK
The scale for both meters (PPM by default). A new scale starts the holds afresh.

REF LEVEL

-9 / -12 / -14 / -15 / -18 / -20
The alignment level in dBFS: the level that reads 0 dB (PPM) or 0 VU. PPM and VU each keep their own setting: PPM -9 dBFS by default (the broadcast convention), VU -18 dBFS. The keys are greyed out on PEAK, which reads dBFS directly.

HOLD

OFF / 2 S / 5 S / INF
How long the hold markers and the PEAK readout hold the highest reading (2 S by default). INF holds until you click a meter or the readout.

Tips

  • For tracking, use VU at -18 dBFS and aim for readings swinging around 0 VU: that leaves plenty of headroom and suits most analogue-modelled plug-ins.
  • Use PEAK with HOLD INF on the master during a final pass, then read the highest peak from the readout.
  • Keep an eye on KORREL. while you widen a mix: if it sits below 0, check the mix in mono.
  • Because the audio is untouched, you can put Meter81 anywhere in a chain - before and after a compressor, for example, to compare levels.
