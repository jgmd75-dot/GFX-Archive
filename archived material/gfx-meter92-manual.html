Mastering

Meter92

Series 92's meter bridge: a pair of backlit VU meters, or a 90s digital meter with overs, bits in use, a goniometer and loudness. Bit-exact.

Overview

Meter92 is the meter bridge of a serious 90s home studio, with two faces on one switch. The audio is never touched: in both models the output is bit-exact with the input and the latency is 0.

ANALOG is a desk's meter bridge: a pair of backlit VU meters with amber-lit faces and true VU ballistics, 0 VU set to -18, -14 or -12 dBFS, and a red peak LED on each meter. DIGITAL is a stand-alone digital meter of the decade: two long plasma-style dBFS peak bars with peak hold and the RMS under them, an OVERS counter that counts runs of consecutive full-scale samples, a bits-in-use display that reads the word length from the signal, a goniometer with a correlation bar, and the short-term loudness (BS.1770) on an LCD.

The meters always run in the background, whichever face is shown. There is no output stage and no DRIFT: a meter must not wander.

Reach for it for

  • Gain staging to a VU reference while tracking and mixing
  • Catching digital overs the way a 90s converter or DAT counted them
  • Checking that a 16-bit or 24-bit file really uses the bits it claims
  • Checking the stereo picture, the correlation and the short-term loudness

Getting started

  1. Insert Meter92 anywhere: it never changes the sound.
  2. On ANALOG, set the VU REFERENCE (0 VU = -18 dBFS is the usual home-studio line-up) and the PEAK LED threshold.
  3. Switch to DIGITAL for the peak bars, the overs, the bits in use, the goniometer and the loudness.
  4. Choose how many consecutive full-scale samples make an over (3 is the usual convention) and the PEAK HOLD.
  5. Press RESET to clear the overs, the hold and the peak maxima.

VU METERS

The ANALOG face.

The meters
Two backlit VU meters, amber-lit faces, black scale -20 to +3 VU with the red zone above 0. True VU ballistics: an average-responding full-wave rectifier into a damped movement that reaches 99% of a step in 300 ms with about 1% overshoot. Calibrated on a sine: a sine whose peak sits at the VU REFERENCE reads 0 VU. Each face prints its reference.
PEAK
A red LED on the plate under each meter: lights for 250 ms when a sample of that channel reaches the PEAK LED threshold.

PEAK · RMS · DBFS

The DIGITAL face.

The bars
One long plasma-style bar per channel, 64 segments from -60 to 0 dBFS (-60 to -30 compressed, the top 30 dB opened out): the sample peak, with an instant rise and a fall of 20 dB in 1.7 s, lit in the lamp colour, yellow from -6 and red from -1 dBFS. The single brighter segment is the peak hold. The thin row under each bar is the RMS (300 ms; a full-scale sine reads -3 dB).
OVER
The lamp at the end of each bar lights for 2 s when that channel counts an over (it stays lit until RESET when PEAK HOLD is on HOLD).
MAX
The highest sample peak of each channel since the last RESET, in dBFS, on a small LCD.

OVERS · BITS

The DIGITAL face.

OVERS
The number of overs on each channel since the last RESET, on two LCDs. An over is a run of N consecutive full-scale samples (at or above 32767 / 32768, the 16-bit full-scale code); each run counts once, however long it is.
RESET
Clears the overs, the peak hold, the OVER lamps and the MAX read-outs.
BITS IN USE
The word length the signal actually uses, read over 0.4 s: a row of bit LEDs (the sign, then the 23 magnitude bits of a 24-bit word from the most significant down, then F) and the result on an LCD: 16 BIT, 20 BIT, 24 BIT (or any other length), FLOAT when any sample is finer than 24 bits, or -- in digital silence. The 16th, 20th and 24th LEDs are yellow; F is red.

PHASE · LOUDNESS

The DIGITAL face.

Goniometer
The last 1536 samples on a backlit screen: mono stands up the M line, the left and right channels alone lie on the L and R diagonals, and out-of-phase material spreads sideways. It ranges itself to the level.
SHORT-TERM
The loudness over the last 3 s per ITU-R BS.1770 (K-weighted), in LUFS, on the LCD; the momentary loudness (400 ms) is printed under it.
CORRELATION
A segment bar from the centre and its value: +1 is mono, 0 is wide or unrelated, and below 0 (red) is out of phase. The RMS of each channel in dBFS is printed underneath.

CALIBRATION

VU REFERENCE
ANALOG: 0 VU = a sine whose peak is at -18, -14 or -12 dBFS (-18). Greyed on DIGITAL.
PEAK LED
ANALOG: the threshold of the peak LEDs, -6, -3 or -1 dBFS sample peak (-3). Greyed on DIGITAL.
PEAK HOLD
DIGITAL: OFF, 3 S (the hold segment falls back after 3 s) or HOLD (held until RESET) (3 S). Greyed on ANALOG.
OVER
DIGITAL: how many consecutive full-scale samples make an over, 1, 2, 3 or 4 (3). Greyed on ANALOG.

Two models

Every Series 92 unit has two models, chosen with the ANALOG / DIGITAL keys in the header - the two halves of a serious 90s home studio. ANALOG is the boutique outboard: valve, opto and transformer colour. DIGITAL is the first affordable 16 / 20-bit converters and DSP: clean, precise and a little glassy. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel lamps glow amber on ANALOG and green on DIGITAL.

ANALOG
A desk's meter bridge: two backlit VU meters with amber-lit faces and true VU ballistics, the VU REFERENCE and a red peak LED on each. The audio is bit-exact.
DIGITAL
A 90s stand-alone digital meter, its lamps green: plasma-style dBFS peak bars with peak hold and RMS, an OVERS counter, bits in use, a goniometer with correlation, and the short-term loudness on an LCD. The audio is bit-exact.

Presets

A preset sets the model, the VU REFERENCE, the PEAK LED, the PEAK HOLD and the OVER count; it does not clear the counters (press RESET). There are six presets, because Meter92 has only five switches. A meter has no loudness to level: the output is bit-exact in every preset.

Init
ANALOG, 0 VU = -18 dBFS, the peak LEDs at -3 dBFS.
Hot Tape -14
ANALOG with 0 VU = -14 dBFS, for hotter levels.
Desk Peaks -12
ANALOG with 0 VU = -12 dBFS and the peak LEDs at -6 dBFS.
DAT Master
DIGITAL: every full-scale sample counts as an over, the peak hold held until RESET.
CD Overs
DIGITAL: overs counted on runs of 3 full-scale samples, the peak hold held until RESET.
Digital Tracking
DIGITAL: every full-scale sample counts as an over, a 3 s hold.
Reset all to defaults
(Last in the list.) Every control back to its starting value.

Tips

  • There is no DRIFT on Meter92: a meter must not wander.
  • At 0 VU = -18 dBFS, keep the VU meters around 0 on loud passages and the digital peaks will sit about 8 to 12 dB under full scale.
  • BITS IN USE shows 16 BIT on a 16-bit file even inside a 24-bit project; a plug-in or a fader move upstream turns it to FLOAT.
  • A goniometer trace leaning towards L or R means the image is lopsided; one lying flat means the channels are out of phase. Check the correlation.
