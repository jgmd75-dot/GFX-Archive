Plate74

Series 74's plate: a heavy remote-studio plate with a valve drive amplifier and an output transformer, or a pure flat-response steel plate, on one switch.

Overview

Plate74 is the plate reverb of Series 74, the OB remote and broadcast series: a heavy sheet of steel in its frame, a driver at one point, two contact pickups and a damper on a screw. It has two models on one switch.

OB is the plate in a remote studio: the send goes through a valve drive amplifier (soft saturation, more with DRIVE) into a dense, bright, slightly metallic plate; the pickups add a little presence, and the balanced output transformer makes the lows bloom. CLEAN is a pure flat-response metal plate: the same tank, fed by a flat line driver, with full bandwidth, flat pickups and a transformerless output.

The plate is fed in mono, as the plates were, and the two pickups give the stereo. The two models are level matched, and the return holds its level across the DAMPER and PLATE settings, so they change the space, not the loudness.

Reach for it for

  • A classic plate on vocals and snare
  • A bright, dense send for drums and percussion
  • Long steel tails on choirs, strings and pads
  • A transparent plate where colour is not wanted

Getting started

  1. Insert Plate74 on a track, or on an FX send with MIX at 100%. It opens on Init: OB, a medium plate, 2.2 s.
  2. Set the decay with DAMPER and pick the plate size with the SMALL, MEDIUM and LARGE lenses.
  3. Add PRE-DELAY to separate the reverb from the source.
  4. Shape the return with LOW CUT and HIGH CUT, and set its spread with WIDTH.
  5. Switch to CLEAN for a flat, uncoloured plate, and set the balance with the MIX fader.

INPUT

IN / OUT meters
Edgewise meters, lit amber on OB and orange-red on CLEAN: the level coming in and the level going out.
TRIM
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.

THE PLATE

The window shows the plate: the sheet grows with the size, the driver (orange) and the two pickups (their spacing follows WIDTH), and the damper's shadow, which comes over the steel as the decay shortens. The strip below reads the decay, the size and the pre-delay.

DAMPER
The damper's position, read as the decay time: 0.7 s (closed) to 5.5 s (open) (2.2 s).
PRE-DELAY
A delay before the plate, 0 - 250 ms (20 ms).
WIDTH
The pickups' spacing, 0 - 100% (80%): 0 is both pickups together (mono), 100% the full width of the plate.
PLATE
SMALL, MEDIUM or LARGE (MEDIUM): a larger plate is a little slower to build and a little darker. The change fades the return out, clears the plate and fades it back in; the dry sound is not touched.

VALVE · IRON

OB only: on CLEAN the drive amplifier and the output transformer are out of the path and the DRIVE label greys.

DRIVE
The valve drive amplifier, 0 - 100% (30%): soft, slightly lopsided saturation of the send, level matched. It also works the output transformer harder, so the lows thicken.
The valve
Its heater glows on OB, brighter as the send drives it; it is dark on CLEAN.

RETURN

The knobs follow the house colour code: LOW CUT has a yellow cap (a high-pass), HIGH CUT a black one (a low-pass). MIX and OUTPUT are desk faders: drag the cap, or click the slot to jump it there; double-click for the default.

LOW CUT
A high-pass on the return, 20 - 400 Hz, 12 dB per octave (100 Hz).
HIGH CUT
A low-pass on the return, 2 - 20 kHz, 12 dB per octave (12 kHz).
MIX
The blue fader: dry to reverb, 0 - 100% (25%). A send law: the dry stays at full up to 50%, the reverb is at full from 50%. Use 100% on an FX send.
OUTPUT
The level fader (dark on OB, cream on CLEAN): the output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
WANDER
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on OB the plate's tension and the pickups' gain wander slowly and the tail breathes; on CLEAN only a trace remains.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.

Two models

Every Series 74 unit has two models, chosen with the OB / CLEAN lenses in the header. OB is the heavy mobile field sound: transformers, saturation and colour. CLEAN is the high-headroom, transparent broadcast path. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel changes with the model: OB is an olive-drab flight case with gunmetal knobs and amber lenses; CLEAN is a broadcast desk, with light-grey modules, red-capped aluminium knobs, cream keys that light red and cream VU windows.

OB
A heavy remote-studio plate: a valve drive amplifier into a dense, bright, slightly metallic plate, pickups with a little presence, and an output transformer that makes the lows bloom.
CLEAN
A pure flat-response metal plate: the same tank fed by a flat line driver, full bandwidth, a little smoother, flat pickups and a transformerless output. DRIVE is out of the path.

Tips

  • Switch between OB and CLEAN on the same settings to hear what the drive amplifier, the pickups and the iron add: the level stays the same.
  • On an FX send, set MIX to 100%: the send law then gives the reverb alone.
  • A little PRE-DELAY (20 - 60 ms) keeps a vocal clear of its plate; more pushes the plate further behind it.
  • For a classic plate on a vocal, raise LOW CUT to 150 - 250 Hz: the plates were usually thinned in the lows.
