Modulation & space

Room74

Series 74's OB ambience: early reflections and a short tail through bucket-brigade lines, or a crystal-clear algorithmic room, on one switch.

Overview

Room74 is the ambience unit from the Series 74 outside-broadcast truck: it puts close-miked sources into a space. Both models build their early reflections from the same geometry: a box room with a listener and the source in front of it. The dry signal is the direct sound, and each reflection off the walls, the floor and the ceiling arrives as much later as its path is longer. SHAPE picks the room, SIZE scales it and DISTANCE moves the source away from the listener.

OB runs the reflections and a short diffuse tail through bucket-brigade lines: an input transformer, a compander, a band limit of about 8.5 kHz and slow clock modulation that smears the reflections like a gentle chorus, then an output transformer. Warm and soft at the top, with no clock noise. CLEAN is a crystal-clear algorithmic room: the same reflection pattern at full bandwidth, then a short, dense, clean tail, with no modulation.

The dry signal is never processed. The two models are level matched, and the room's level follows DISTANCE rather than SIZE, SHAPE or DECAY. There is no latency.

Reach for it for

  • Putting close-miked vocals, speech and instruments into a believable room
  • Commentary-box, studio, hall or stadium ambience for broadcast and post work
  • Warm, smeared early reflections that thicken without a long tail
  • Clean, natural rooms that leave the source in front

Getting started

  1. Insert Room74 on a track. It opens on Init: OB, a studio room, the source a little way off.
  2. Pick the room with the BOOTH, STUDIO, HALL and STADIUM lenses, and set SIZE.
  3. Turn DISTANCE up to move the source further away: more room, less direct sound.
  4. Set DECAY for the tail, and on OB set SMEAR for the modulation on the reflections.
  5. Switch to CLEAN for a clear, unmodulated room, then balance with MIX and set the level with OUTPUT.

INPUT

IN / OUT meters
Edgewise meters, lit amber on OB and orange-red on CLEAN: the level coming in and the level going out.
TRIM
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.

ROOM

BOOTH / STUDIO / HALL / STADIUM
The room: its proportions, how hard its walls are, the size of its tail and the air. BOOTH is small and dead; STUDIO a live room; HALL large and even; STADIUM open above, with long, dark slap-backs from the stands (STUDIO by default). Changing it fades the room out and back in; the dry signal carries on.
The plan
A backlit plan view of the room: the walls, the listener (the ring), the source (the orange dot), the direct sound and the four wall reflections. Below it, the room's size in metres, the source's distance and the decay.
SIZE
The room from 0.6x to 1.4x its shape's size, 0 - 100% (50%). Larger rooms spread the reflections out.
DISTANCE
The source's distance from the listener, 0 - 100% (35%). Close: the direct sound leads and the reflections come late and low. Far: the reflections close up on the direct sound and the room and its tail rise.
DECAY
The tail's reverberation time, 0.1 - 3 s (0.6 s). The level stays steady as it changes.

BBD · SMEAR

OB only: on CLEAN SMEAR is out of the path and its label greys. The lamp breathes with the modulation.

SMEAR
How far the bucket-brigade clocks are swept, 0 - 100% (35%): a slow, chorus-like smear on the reflections and a little movement in the tail. The note below shows the bucket brigade's bandwidth, set by TONE.

OUTPUT

TONE has the black cap of the house colour code (it acts as a low-pass on the top); MIX and OUTPUT are desk faders.

TONE
Dark to bright, -100 to +100 (0): on OB the bucket brigade's bandwidth (about 6 to 12 kHz) and the tail's damping; on CLEAN the air on the reflections and the tail's damping.
WIDTH
The spread of the reflections and the tail's stereo, mono to wide, 0 - 100% (80%).
MIX
The blue-capped fader: dry to room, 0 - 100% (50%). A send law: the dry signal stays at full up to 50% and the room is at full from 50%, so 50% is the dry signal with the room added.
OUTPUT
The cream-capped fader: the output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
WANDER
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on OB the bucket brigade's clock and the smear's LFOs wander slowly; on CLEAN only a trace of clock wander remains.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.

Two models

Every Series 74 unit has two models, chosen with the OB / CLEAN lenses in the header. OB is the heavy mobile field sound: transformers, saturation and colour. CLEAN is the high-headroom, transparent broadcast path. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel changes with the model: OB is an olive-drab flight case with gunmetal knobs and amber lenses; CLEAN is a broadcast desk, with light-grey modules, red-capped aluminium knobs, cream keys that light red and cream VU windows.

OB
The reflections and a short tail through companded bucket-brigade lines, between an input and an output transformer: band-limited, warm and slowly smeared (SMEAR). No clock noise.
CLEAN
A crystal-clear algorithmic room: the geometric reflection pattern at full bandwidth, then a short, dense, clean tail. No modulation; SMEAR is out of the path.

Presets

A preset sets SHAPE, SIZE, DISTANCE, DECAY, SMEAR, TONE, WIDTH, MIX, OUTPUT and the model; TRIM, GUARD, WANDER and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Tips

  • DISTANCE is the main control for how much room you hear: it changes the level of the room and the gap between the direct sound and the first reflections together, as moving a microphone back would.
  • Switch between OB and CLEAN on the same room to hear what the bucket brigade adds: the reflections are the same, the level stays the same.
  • For thickness without an obvious reverb, use BOOTH or STUDIO with a short DECAY and some SMEAR on OB.
  • With MIX at 100% Room74 returns only the room, for use on a send.
