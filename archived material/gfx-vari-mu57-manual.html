Dynamics

Vari-Mu57

Series 57's compressor: a variable-mu valve pair whose ratio rises as it works, six time constants, stereo, lateral / vertical or dual.

Overview

Vari-Mu57 is the Series 57 counterpart of Fet81. There were no FETs in 1957, so the gain element is a push-pull pair of remote-cutoff valves: as the side-chain pushes their grid bias negative, their gain (mu) falls.

There is no ratio control. The ratio rises with the gain reduction, as on the valves: about 1.5:1 just over the threshold, past 6:1 when it is pushed hard. BIAS sets how quickly it rises. The side-chain hears the output (feedback), and six TIME CONSTANT positions cover everything from quick levelling to slow, programme-dependent recovery.

MODE runs the two channels linked (STEREO), as mid and side (LAT / VERT, as on a disc-cutting lathe) or independently (DUAL). The audio passes the valve pair and an output transformer at 2x oversampling.

Reach for it for

  • Gentle glue on a mix bus or a group
  • Smooth levelling on vocals and bass
  • Programme limiting with a soft, musical knee
  • Mid / side control on a master
  • Parallel valve compression on drums

Getting started

  1. Insert Vari-Mu57 on a track or bus. It opens on Init: about 3 - 4 dB of reduction on a typical mix.
  2. Lower THRESHOLD until the GAIN REDUCTION needle moves as much as you want.
  3. Pick a TIME CONSTANT: 1 - 2 for quick levelling, 3 - 4 for slower bus control, 5 - 6 for programme-dependent recovery.
  4. Use BIAS to make it gentler or firmer, and OUTPUT to match the level.
  5. Try LAT / VERT on a stereo mix to control the middle and the sides separately.

GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.

GAIN REDUCTION

The meter
An edgewise meter: the needle rests at 0 on the right and swings left as it compresses.
STEREO / LAT/VERT / DUAL
STEREO: both channels compressed together. LAT / VERT: the middle (lateral) and the sides (vertical) each with their own valve pair. DUAL: two independent channels. Changing it fades out and back in.
THRESHOLD
Where it starts, -40 to 0 dB (-18 dB).
TIME CONSTANT
Six positions (2): 1 - 0.2 ms / 0.3 s; 2 - 0.2 ms / 0.8 s; 3 - 0.4 ms / 2 s; 4 - 0.4 ms / 5 s; 5 - 0.4 ms / programme: 2 s, and 10 s after long passages; 6 - 0.2 ms / programme: 0.3 s, 10 s and 25 s.
BIAS
How quickly the ratio rises with the reduction, SOFT to FIRM (50%). SOFT stays gentle; FIRM leans towards a limiter.

SIDE-CHAIN · OUTPUT

SC FILTER
A high-pass in the side-chain, OFF or 20 - 300 Hz: the lows push it less.
MIX
Dry to compressed, 0 - 100% (100%). The dry signal is delayed to match.
OUTPUT
The make-up, -12 to +24 dB (+3.6 dB).
DRIFT
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): the valves' bias wanders - the threshold and the balance of the pair - and the two channels never quite match.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). As a mix tool it has no clip stage; the red LED lights on an over.
The valve
Glows brighter the harder it works.

Two faces

Every Series 57 unit has two faces, chosen with the ULM / COPENHAGEN keys in the header. Each changes the look and the sound, and the two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. Presets leave the face as it is.

ULM
Quicker, with a firmer knee; the push-pull pair balanced (odd harmonics, clean) and a breath of air on top.
COPENHAGEN
Slower (about twice the attack time), a softer knee; the pair a little unbalanced (even harmonics, warmer), a fuller iron low end and a softer top.

Presets

A preset sets THRESHOLD, TIME CONSTANT, BIAS, MODE, SC FILTER, OUTPUT and MIX; the face, INPUT, GUARD, DRIFT and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Init
Moderate levelling: threshold -18 dB, time constant 2.
Gentle Bus
Light, slow bus control with the side-chain filtered.
Mix Glue
Programme-dependent recovery (5) for a whole mix.
Vocal Leveller
Dual channels, firmer, quick recovery.
Programme Limiter
High threshold, firm bias, fastest recovery: catches peaks.
Drum Squash
Pushed hard and fast.
Bass Hold
Steady bass with a 5 s recovery.
Lat-Vert Master
Mid and side compressed separately, time constant 6.
Slow Swell
Soft bias and a slow recovery: the level breathes.
Parallel Valves
Hard compression mixed in at 40%.
Pumping Room
Fast and filtered: the room pumps with the beat.
Brick Wall

Reset all to defaults
(Last in the list.) Every control back to its starting value; the face stays.

Tips

  • Because the ratio rises with the reduction, a little goes a long way: aim for 2 - 4 dB on a bus.
  • Time constants 5 and 6 recover quickly after a short peak and slowly after a long loud passage: ideal for whole mixes.
  • On a master, LAT / VERT lets you hold the middle steady while the sides breathe.
  • COPENHAGEN is slower and warmer: lovely on vocals and acoustic instruments.
