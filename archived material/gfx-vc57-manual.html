VC57

Series 57's vocoder: the valve channel vocoder of the late-50s electronic music studios - 10, 14 or 20 bands, a buzz generator played from MIDI, or your own carrier.

Overview

VC57 is the Series 57 counterpart of VC81: the valve channel vocoder of the electronic music studios of the late 50s. A bank of band-pass filters analyses the voice, a rectifier and a smoothing filter on each band follow its level, and the same bank, fed with a buzz or any other sound, is opened band by band to match.

The voice goes in on channels 1/2. The carrier is the BUZZ, the studio's relaxation generator played from MIDI (six voices), or EXT, anything you route to channels 3/4. The bank has 10, 14 or 20 fourth-order bands from 120 Hz to 7.5 kHz.

Reach for it for

  • Classic robot and choir vocoding
  • Talking synths, organs and pads (EXT)
  • Whispered, breathy textures (HISS)
  • Larger or smaller throats with SHIFT

Getting started

  1. Insert VC57 on the vocal track. It opens on Init: the BUZZ carrier, 14 bands.
  2. Arm the track for MIDI as well (or add a MIDI item) and play a chord while the voice plays: the BUZZ sings with the voice.
  3. Set RESPONSE for how quickly it follows, and EMPHASIS and SIBILANCE for clarity.
  4. For EXT, route a synth or any sound into channels 3/4 of this track and press EXT.

CARRIER

The carrier: EXT (channels 3/4 of the track) or BUZZ (played from MIDI). Changing it fades out and back in.
MIDI
Lights while BUZZ voices are sounding.
HOLD
Keeps the chord sounding after the keys are up, until the next chord.
SHAPE
From a round sawtooth (SAW) to a narrow, very rich pulse train (BUZZ), 0 - 100% (40%). Kept about level.
TUNE
-12 to +12 semitones (0).
OCTAVE
-1, 0 or +1.
GLIDE
Each new note slides from the last, 0 - 100% (0).
HISS
A breath of noise in the carrier, for the unvoiced sounds, 0 - 100% (15%).

FILTER BANK

10 / 14 / 20
The number of bands. Changing it fades out and back in.
The band lamps
One bar per band, lit by the voice.
RESPONSE
How quickly the bands follow the voice, 8 - 60 Hz smoothing (25 Hz): slow and smooth to quick and clear.
EMPHASIS
Lifts the voice's top before the analysis, 0 - 100% (40%): more articulate.
SIBILANCE
Passes the voice's own consonants (above 5 kHz) straight through, 0 - 100% (30%).
SHIFT
Moves the carrier's bands against the voice's, -6 to +6 bands (0): a larger (-) or smaller (+) throat.

IN · OUT

VOICE
The voice's trim, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard on the voice (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.
MIX
From the voice (0%) to the vocoder (100%; the default).
OUTPUT
The output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
DRIFT
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): the buzz generator's pitch wanders and the valve filters' centres drift a little, each on its own.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.

MIDI

Notes play the BUZZ (six voices; velocity sets each voice's level). Pitch bend +/- 2 semitones; CC120 / CC123 release all notes. MIDI is passed on.

The signal path

The line along the foot: VOICE and CARRIER, ANALYSIS, SYNTHESIS, OUT.

Two faces

Every Series 57 unit has two faces, chosen with the ULM / COPENHAGEN keys in the header. Each changes the look and the sound, and the two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. Presets leave the face as it is.

ULM
Steeper, narrower bands and quicker followers: precise and articulate.
COPENHAGEN
Wider, overlapping bands and softer followers: warmer and more blended.

Presets

A preset sets the carrier, the BUZZ controls, the bank and MIX; the face, HOLD, VOICE, GUARD, OUTPUT, DRIFT and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness with the BUZZ.

Init
The BUZZ, 14 bands, moderate response.
Studio Voice
20 bands, bright and articulate.
Buzz Choir
A round carrier, slow response, a little glide.
Ten-Channel Classic
Ten bands, as on the earliest vocoders.
Twenty-Channel Clarity
20 bands, quick and clear.
Robot Announcer
A narrow buzz an octave down.
Deep Throat
Shifted down four bands, an octave down.
Small Throat
Shifted up four bands.
Whisper
Mostly hiss: a whispered voice.
Soft Ensemble
A slow, round, gliding carrier, mixed a little with the voice.
External Carrier
EXT: your own carrier on channels 3/4.
Organ Talk
A round carrier an octave up, shifted up two bands.
Reset all to defaults
(Last in the list.) Every control back to its starting value; the face stays.

Tips

  • Hold sustained chords: the vocoder speaks only while the carrier sounds.
  • A rich carrier (SHAPE towards BUZZ, or a bright synth on EXT) makes the words clearer.
  • Ten bands sound like the 1950s; twenty bands are far more intelligible.
  • COPENHAGEN blends into a soft choir; ULM keeps every consonant.
