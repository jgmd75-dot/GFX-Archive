Modulation & space

VC74

Series 74's field vocoder: 24 gritty, drifting bands through transformers, or a sharp, matched bank that keeps every consonant, on one switch.

Overview

VC74 is the vocoder of Series 74, the OB remote and broadcast series. A bank of 24 band-pass filters analyses the voice, a follower on each band tracks its level, and the same bank, fed with the carrier, is opened band by band to match.

OB is the field unit from the truck: an input transformer, 24 wide, overlapping bands with a rectifier and a single smoothing pole on each (a little ripple is left in), a carrier bank that never quite matches the voice's, a band of noise for the sibilants and a driven output transformer. CLEAN is a transparent, high-articulation processor: 24 sharp bands, the voice's and the carrier's exactly matched, fast ripple-free followers, and an unvoiced detector that finds the consonants and passes them straight through. It adds no harmonics.

The voice goes in on channels 1/2. The carrier is INT, played from MIDI (six voices: BUZZ, SAW, NOISE or CHORD), or EXT, anything you route to channels 3/4. The two models are level matched.

Reach for it for

  • Robot voices and talkback-style vocoding
  • Talking synths, organs and pads (EXT)
  • Choirs and chords sung by a voice (CHORD)
  • Clear, intelligible vocoding where the words must come through (CLEAN)

Getting started

  1. Insert VC74 on the vocal track. It opens on Init: OB, the INT carrier on BUZZ.
  2. Arm the track for MIDI as well (or add a MIDI item) and play a chord while the voice plays: the carrier sings with the voice.
  3. Set ATTACK and RELEASE for how quickly it follows, and EMPHASIS and SIBILANCE for clarity.
  4. Switch to CLEAN for a sharper, more articulate sound, and move FORMANT to change the size of the throat.
  5. For EXT, route a synth or any sound into channels 3/4 of this track and press EXT.

CARRIER

MIDI
Lights while notes are sounding. MIDI is passed on to the plug-ins after VC74.
HOLD
Keeps the notes sounding after the keys are up, until the next ones (off by default).
WAVE
The internal carrier: BUZZ (a narrow pulse train, very rich), SAW (a sawtooth), NOISE (white noise; it sounds without notes, for a whisper) or CHORD (each key plays its root, fifth and octave). The change fades out and back in.
TUNE

OCTAVE
Down one, 0 or up one octave (0).
GLIDE
How long a new note slides from the last, 0 - 100% (0%).
On EXT

FILTER BANK · 24 BANDS

The band lamps show the level of each of the 24 bands, 100 Hz to 8 kHz: amber bars in a dark window on OB, charcoal bars in a window lit orange-red on CLEAN.

FORMANT
Moves the carrier's bands against the voice's, -12 to +12 semitones (0): down for a larger throat, up for a smaller one. The carrier's pitch is not touched.
ATTACK
How quickly the bands open, 0.5 - 50 ms (4 ms).
RELEASE
How quickly they close, 10 - 500 ms (60 ms): short for crisp words, long for a smoother, sustained sound.
EMPHASIS
Lifts the voice's top before the analysis, 0 - 100% (40%): more articulate and brighter. Its cap is red, the house colour for the high frequencies.
SIBILANCE
The consonants, 0 - 100% (40%). On OB a band of noise (3 - 9 kHz) opened by the voice's top; on CLEAN the unvoiced detector passes the voice's own consonants straight through and turns the carrier towards noise while they last.

OUTPUT

VOICE meter
An edgewise meter, lit amber on OB and orange-red on CLEAN: the level of the voice coming in.
TRIM
Trim on the voice, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
WANDER
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on OB the centres of the two banks drift, each band on its own, and the carrier's pitch wanders; on CLEAN only a trace of pitch wander remains.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot voices down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.
MIX
A fader with a blue cap: the voice (0%) to the vocoder (100%) (100%).
OUTPUT
A fader with a cream cap: the output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).

Two models

Every Series 74 unit has two models, chosen with the OB / CLEAN lenses in the header. OB is the heavy mobile field sound: transformers, saturation and colour. CLEAN is the high-headroom, transparent broadcast path. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel changes with the model: OB is an olive-drab flight case with gunmetal knobs and amber lenses; CLEAN is a broadcast desk, with light-grey modules, red-capped aluminium knobs, cream keys that light red and cream VU windows.

OB
The field vocoder: an input transformer, 24 wide, overlapping bands, a little off between the voice and the carrier and drifting with WANDER, followers with a little ripple, a band of noise for the sibilants and a driven output transformer. Gritty and blended.
CLEAN
A transparent, high-articulation vocoder: 24 sharp bands exactly matched, fast ripple-free followers, and an unvoiced detector that passes the consonants. No added harmonics.

Presets

A preset sets the carrier, the wave, TUNE, OCTAVE, GLIDE, FORMANT, ATTACK, RELEASE, EMPHASIS, SIBILANCE, MIX, OUTPUT and the model; TRIM, GUARD, HOLD, WANDER and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Tips

  • The voice should be dry and fairly even: compress it before VC74, and keep its reverb after.
  • If the words are hard to follow, switch to CLEAN, shorten RELEASE and raise SIBILANCE and EMPHASIS.
  • FORMANT changes the apparent size of the voice without touching the carrier's pitch: down for giants, up for small radios.
  • NOISE needs no MIDI at all: use it for a whispered double under the dry voice, with MIX below 100%.
