Modulation & space

VRB57

Series 57's reverb: the live studio, a concert hall borrowed as a chamber, or the organ's two-spring unit, behind a valve send amplifier.

Overview

VRB57 is the Series 57 counterpart of VRB81: the reverberation a late-50s studio could reach for, behind a valve send amplifier.

STUDIO is the live room, wood and plaster, with strong early reflections and a short, warm tail. HALL is a concert hall borrowed as a chamber: a long, even, darker tail with a slow build. SPRING is the organ's two-spring reverb unit; the springs' dispersion gives the "drip", the chirp of a sharp sound, and TENSION sets how much they drip.

Reach for it for

  • Natural rooms on vocals, drums and instruments
  • Long, dark hall tails
  • Spring reverb on guitars and organs
  • Surf and rockabilly drip

Getting started

  1. Insert VRB57 on a track, or on a send with MIX at 100%. It opens on Init: a medium studio.
  2. Pick STUDIO, HALL or SPRING.
  3. Set DECAY for the length and PRE-DELAY to separate it from the source; the window draws the decay.
  4. Shape it with DAMP (the top) and LOW (the bottom); WIDTH sets the stereo spread.
  5. Push DRIVE for a gritty valve send, especially on the springs.

The window
STUDIO and HALL: the decay drawn in dB against time - the pre-delay gap, the early reflections, the tail. SPRING: the two springs, trembling with the level.

DECAY

PRE-DELAY
0 - 200 ms (12 ms).
DECAY
0.3 - 8 s (1.6 s); with SPRING, how long the springs ring.
SIZE / TENSION
The room's size (STUDIO, HALL), or the springs' drip (SPRING), 0 - 100% (50%).
DAMP
The top dies faster: the air and the plaster, 0 - 100% (40%).
LOW
The low end's decay against the middle's, -100 to +100 (0).
WIDTH
The two microphones (or springs) from mono to wide, 0 - 100% (70%).

IN · OUT

INPUT
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.
DRIVE
The valve send amplifier, 0 - 100% (20%). Oversampled 2x.
MIX
Dry to reverb, 0 - 100% (25%). The dry stays at full level up to 50%.
OUTPUT
The output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
DRIFT
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): the microphones and the springs' hangers move a hair - the tail shimmers very slightly.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). As a mix tool it passes straight through otherwise.

The signal path

The line along the foot: IN, SEND AMP, ROOM (or SPRINGS), OUT.

Two faces

Every Series 57 unit has two faces, chosen with the ULM / COPENHAGEN keys in the header. Each changes the look and the sound, and the two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. Presets leave the face as it is.

ULM
Brighter, denser and clearer.
COPENHAGEN
Darker and softer, with a little more low-mid warmth; the springs drip more gently.

Presets

A preset sets the algorithm, PRE-DELAY, DECAY, SIZE / TENSION, DAMP, LOW, WIDTH, DRIVE and MIX; the face, INPUT, GUARD, OUTPUT, DRIFT and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Tips

  • On a send, set MIX to 100%.
  • Snare and guitar through SPRING with high TENSION give the classic drip; lower it for a smoother spring.
  • A PRE-DELAY of 20 - 40 ms keeps a vocal clear in front of the room.
  • COPENHAGEN's darker rooms sit further back in a mix.
