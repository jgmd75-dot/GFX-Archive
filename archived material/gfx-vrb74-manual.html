Modulation & space

VRB74

Series 74's OB reverb: a vintage algorithmic unit with a chorused, grainy tail, or a pristine eight-line room simulator, on one switch.

Overview

VRB74 is the remote space unit of Series 74, the OB remote and broadcast series: units built like the flight cases on an outside-broadcast truck. It is the reverb, with two models on one switch.

OB is a vintage algorithmic reverb: the send passes through an input transformer, is band-limited to about 10 kHz and sampled at a reduced rate by a 12-bit converter, then circulates in a figure-eight all-pass loop of modest density whose delays are audibly chorused. The tail comes back through a 12-bit converter and an output transformer: characterful, a little grainy, with sparse early reflections. CLEAN is a pristine acoustic room simulator: early reflections, input diffusion and an eight-line feedback delay network with a Hadamard matrix, its low, middle and high bands each with their own reverb time, a trace of modulation and the full bandwidth.

Four SPACE lenses pick the kind of space - ROOM, HALL, CHAMBER or CATHEDRAL - and SIZE sets it within its kind. The wet is level-normalised against DECAY and SIZE, and the two models are matched to each other, so the switch changes the character, not the loudness.

Reach for it for

  • A vintage digital hall or chamber with a chorused tail on vocals, keys and pads
  • A natural, transparent room or hall for speech, choirs and acoustic recordings
  • Shaping the tail by band: a warm low end, a darker or brighter top
  • Long cathedral spaces and chorused washes

Getting started

  1. Insert VRB74 on a track or a send. It opens on Init: OB, a hall of 1.8 s, MIX at 25%.
  2. Pick the space with the four lenses, then set SIZE and DECAY.
  3. Shape the tail with LOW RT and HIGH RT; the window shows the three bands dying away.
  4. Switch to CLEAN for a transparent room simulator; on OB, turn up MOD for more chorus.
  5. Set the blend with MIX (or set it to 100% on a send) and the level with OUTPUT.

INPUT

IN / OUT meters
Edgewise meters, lit amber on OB and orange-red on CLEAN: the level coming in and the level going out.
TRIM
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.

SPACE

The note under the window says what the model is doing: REDUCED RATE · 12-BIT · IRON on OB, EIGHT-LINE FDN · FULL BAND on CLEAN.

ROOM / HALL / CHAMBER / CATHEDRAL
The kind of space (HALL): the lengths of the lines, the pattern and spread of the early reflections and the crossovers of the three bands. ROOM is small with strong early reflections; CHAMBER dense and bright; HALL long and even; CATHEDRAL very long and dark with a slow build. The change fades the wet out and back in.
The window
The decay drawn: the pre-delay gap, the early reflections (their height follows EARLY) and three lines falling to -60 dB - the middle band bold, the LOW and HIGH bands finer. The space and DECAY are printed at the top, the time scale along the bottom.

TAIL

The three bands' reverb times carry the house colour code: LOW RT brown, DECAY (the middle band) green, HIGH RT red.

PRE-DELAY
The gap before the reverb, 0 - 250 ms (20 ms). It glides, so it can be moved while playing.
SIZE
The space within its kind, 0 - 100% (50%): from 0.6x to 1.4x the lines' lengths and the early spread. It glides slowly, with a soft pitch swoop rather than a click.
DECAY
The middle band's reverb time (RT60), 0.2 - 12 s (1.8 s).
LOW RT
The low band's reverb time as a multiple of DECAY, 0.5x - 2x (1.2x): above 1 the lows ring on, below 1 they die first.
HIGH RT
The high band's reverb time as a multiple of DECAY, 0.1x - 1x (0.55x): lower is darker, as air and soft walls take the top away.
DIFFUSION
How fast the echoes smear into a wash, 0 - 100% (70%): low is grainier, with distinct repeats; high is smooth.
MOD
The chorusing of the tail, 0 - 100% (40%). On OB an audible chorus, faster and deeper as it rises; on CLEAN only a slow trace that keeps the tail from ringing.
EARLY
The level of the early reflections, 0 - 100% (50%). CLEAN has twelve a side; OB a sparser six.
WIDTH
The wet's stereo width, 0 - 150% (100%): 0 is mono, above 100% wider than the space.

OUTPUT

MIX and OUTPUT are desk faders: drag the cap, or click the slot to jump it there; double-click for the default.

MIX
The blue-capped fader: dry to wet, 0 - 100% (25%), as a send (SEND-LAW MIX): the dry stays at full up to 50% and the wet reaches full at 50%. Set it to 100% on a send.
OUTPUT
The output fader: the output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
WANDER
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on OB the chorus's rate and depth and the loop's delays wander slowly; on CLEAN only a trace remains.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.

Two models

Every Series 74 unit has two models, chosen with the OB / CLEAN lenses in the header. OB is the heavy mobile field sound: transformers, saturation and colour. CLEAN is the high-headroom, transparent broadcast path. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel changes with the model: OB is an olive-drab flight case with gunmetal knobs and amber lenses; CLEAN is a broadcast desk, with light-grey modules, red-capped aluminium knobs, cream keys that light red and cream VU windows.

OB
A vintage algorithmic reverb: transformers in and out, a 12-bit converter at a reduced rate with about 10 kHz of bandwidth, and a figure-eight all-pass loop with audible chorus. A characterful, slightly grainy tail with sparse early reflections.
CLEAN
A pristine acoustic room simulator: twelve early reflections a side, input diffusion and an eight-line feedback delay network with a separate reverb time for the lows, the middle and the highs. Full bandwidth, very low modulation, WANDER much smaller.

Tips

  • Switch between OB and CLEAN with the same settings to hear what the converters, the transformers and the chorused loop add: the level stays the same.
  • For speech, keep DECAY short, EARLY up and HIGH RT near 1x: the room is heard, not the tail.
  • On OB, low DIFFUSION and high MOD give the grainy, swimming tails of the early digital units; high DIFFUSION and little MOD a smoother hall.
  • On a send, set MIX to 100% and use PRE-DELAY to keep the dry sound clear of the tail.
