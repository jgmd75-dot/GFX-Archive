Modulation & space

VRB92

Series 92's studio reverb: a valve-driven spring tank with its chirpy drip, or the workhorse 90s digital reverb, on one switch.

Overview

VRB92 is the main reverb of Series 92: high-end home recording from the 90s, the rack outboard and DSP boxes of a serious home studio of the decade. It has two models on one switch.

ANALOG is the boutique valve spring unit of the time. The send passes through a valve stage (DRIVE) and the tank's band limits into the driver coil, which DWELL drives harder or softer, and on into two or three springs. Each spring is dispersive: every echo comes back as the chirpy spring drip, more smeared on each pass, and a hard hit makes the springs BOING. TENSION runs from loose, long and very drippy to tight, short and brighter. The springs return through a valve recovery stage. DIGITAL is the workhorse digital reverb of the decade: HALL, ROOM, PLATE and CHAMBER programs with PRE-DELAY, SIZE, DIFFUSION, a touch of chorus (MOD) and a three-band decay, built on an eight-line feedback delay network. CONVERTER picks VINTAGE (about 16 kHz of bandwidth, 16 bits) or STUDIO (full band, 20 bits). The LCD shows the program and its decay.

The wet is level-normalised against DECAY, SIZE, TENSION, DWELL and DRIVE, and the two models are matched to each other at the default MIX, so the switch and the knobs change the space, not the loudness.

Reach for it for

  • The splash and drip of a spring tank on guitar, organ and electric piano
  • Dub-style spring splashes and boings on snares and stabs
  • A smooth hall or plate on vocals
  • Rooms and chambers for drums and acoustic instruments
  • The slightly glassy 16-bit tails of the 90s

Getting started

  1. Insert VRB92 on a track, or on a send with MIX at 100%. It opens on Init: ANALOG, three springs, 2 s of decay, MIX at 25%.
  2. Set TENSION for the springs' character and DWELL for how hard the send hits the tank; DRIVE warms the valves.
  3. Set DECAY for the length of the tail and HIGH RT for its brightness.
  4. Switch to DIGITAL and pick a program (HALL, ROOM, PLATE or CHAMBER); shape it with PRE-DELAY, SIZE, DIFFUSION and MOD.
  5. Blend with the MIX fader and set the level with the OUTPUT fader.

Controls

The header

PRESETS
Opens the preset list (a click on the preset display does the same). The list ends with Reset all to defaults.
◄ / ►
Step to the previous or next preset.
Preset display
Shows the name of the preset in use. New instances open on Init.
ANALOG / DIGITAL
The model: the character of the unit (see Two models). Presets may set it.
S / M / L
Panel size: small, medium or large. The panel also scales to fit the plugin window.
?
Opens a pop-up with the design credit, the GFX website address and the address of this manual.

INPUT

IN / OUT ladders
LED ladders, -30 to +6 (0 = 0 VU): the level going in and the level coming out.
TRIM
Trim before everything, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).
GUARD
The house input guard: eases very hot sources down (on by default). Its LED lights while it works.

SPRING TANK

ANALOG only: the section's captions grey on DIGITAL, where its controls are out of the path.

TENSION
The springs' tension, 0 - 100% (50%): loose springs are long, slow and very dispersive (the most drip); tight ones are shorter and brighter with less drip. It glides, so it can be moved while the tank rings.
DWELL
How hard the send drives the tank's coil, 0 - 100% (50%): more DWELL saturates the driver and makes hits BOING harder. The level is held.
DRIVE
The valve send and recovery stages, 0 - 100% (35%): from clean to warm and thick. The level is held.
SPRINGS
Two or three springs (3). With two, one spring feeds each side; with three, the third is shared between them for a denser, wider tank. The change fades the wet out and back in.

PROGRAM

DIGITAL only: the section's captions grey on ANALOG, where its controls are out of the path.

HALL / ROOM / PLATE / CHAMBER
The program (HALL): the lengths of the network's lines, the early reflections and the crossovers of the decay. PLATE has no early reflections, more diffusion and a brighter decay. The change fades the wet out and back in.
PRE-DELAY
The gap before the reverb, 0 - 250 ms (20 ms).
SIZE
The space within its program, 0 - 100% (50%): 0.6x to 1.4x the program's line lengths. It glides.
DIFFUSION
How fast the echoes smear into a wash, 0 - 100% (75%).
MOD
A touch of chorus on four of the network's lines, 0 - 100% (30%): it softens the tail's ringing; high settings give a lush, swimming wash.
CONVERTER
VINTAGE or STUDIO (VINTAGE). VINTAGE band-limits the send and the return to about 16 kHz and returns the wet at 16 bits with TPDF dither; STUDIO is full band at 20 bits. The change fades the wet out and back in.

DECAY

The LCD shows the program and its decay on DIGITAL, the number of springs and the tank's decay on ANALOG. The knobs are colour coded: DECAY green (the middle band), LOW RT brown, HIGH RT red.

DECAY
The reverb time (RT60 of the middle band), 0.2 - 12 s (2 s). On ANALOG the tank's decay, held to 0.4 - 6 s.
LOW RT
DIGITAL only: the low band's reverb time as a multiple of DECAY, 0.5x - 2x (1.2x). Greyed on ANALOG.
HIGH RT
The high band's reverb time as a multiple of DECAY, 0.1x - 1x (0.55x): lower is darker. On ANALOG it sets the tank's high damping.

OUTPUT

DRIFT
The series' drift, 0 - 100% (50% is the standard amount): on ANALOG the valves warm up and the mains wander, so the springs' tension and the valves' bias drift slowly, every spring on its own; on DIGITAL the clock is steady and only a trace remains in the chorus.
SAT
The house output stage's band saturation (off by default). Its soft CLIP is always on; the red LED lights when it catches a peak.
MIX
A fader with a blue cap: dry to wet, 0 - 100% (25%), with the send law - the dry stays at full up to 50% while the wet comes up, then the dry goes down. At 100% the output is all wet, for use on a send.
OUTPUT
A fader: the output level, -24 to +12 dB (0 dB).

Two models

Every Series 92 unit has two models, chosen with the ANALOG / DIGITAL keys in the header - the two halves of a serious 90s home studio. ANALOG is the boutique outboard: valve, opto and transformer colour. DIGITAL is the first affordable 16 / 20-bit converters and DSP: clean, precise and a little glassy. The two are level matched; switching fades the sound out and back in. The panel lamps glow amber on ANALOG and green on DIGITAL.

ANALOG
A valve-driven spring tank: two or three dispersive springs with the chirpy drip and the BOING on hits, the tank's narrow band, a valve send and return. TENSION, DWELL, DRIVE and SPRINGS shape it; PRE-DELAY, SIZE, LOW RT, DIFFUSION, MOD, the programs and the converter are out of the path.
DIGITAL
The workhorse 90s digital reverb: HALL, ROOM, PLATE and CHAMBER programs on an eight-line network with a three-band decay and a touch of chorus, through a VINTAGE 16 kHz / 16-bit or a STUDIO full-band / 20-bit converter. TENSION, DWELL, DRIVE and SPRINGS are out of the path.

Presets

A preset sets PRE-DELAY, SIZE, DECAY, LOW RT, HIGH RT, DIFFUSION, MOD, the program, TENSION, DWELL, DRIVE, SPRINGS, MIX, OUTPUT, the converter and the model; TRIM, GUARD, DRIFT and the output stage are left as they are. The presets are levelled to play at a similar loudness.

Tips

  • Switch between ANALOG and DIGITAL at the same MIX to hear spring against hall: the level stays the same.
  • For the classic spring drip, turn TENSION down and DWELL up, and feed the tank something percussive.
  • On DIGITAL, VINTAGE gives the slightly soft, glassy top of the 16-bit units; STUDIO opens the full band.
  • On a send, set MIX to 100% and use PRE-DELAY (DIGITAL) to keep the dry sound clear of the tail.
